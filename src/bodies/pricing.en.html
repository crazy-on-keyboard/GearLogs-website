How billing works

No surprises, by design

B.1

Per person, honestly

You pay for people who can sign in — not for rows in a table. Five accounts come with the base plan; beyond them, each extra person is a flat $39 a month. Disable a member and their seat frees on the spot; re-enabling simply checks the limit again. Adding seats mid-period bills only the prorated remainder; reducing seats settles at your next renewal. You subscribe and pay by card inside the app, from Settings › Billing; the payment window is Paddle’s and card details never touch GearLogs.

B.2

Priority Operations, if you want it

An optional add-on at $99 a month ($990 a year): we reply first to your support requests, within 4 business hours (Sunday–Thursday 09:00–18:00, Israel time). Switch it on or off from Settings › Billing at any time — the prorated amount is shown before anything is billed, and switching off credits the unused time to your next invoice. Everything else in GearLogs is the same with it or without it.

B.3

Billed by Paddle

Paddle is our merchant of record: they process payment, apply the correct taxes, and issue your invoices. Card details are entered on Paddle's secure pages and never touch GearLogs.

B.4

Leave without a fight

Cancel yourself, any time — it takes effect at the period end, and one click undoes it before then. Afterwards your workspace stays read-only and fully exportable for 12 months, then deleted with at least 30 days’ notice by email. Your records were always yours; see the refund policy for the rest.
