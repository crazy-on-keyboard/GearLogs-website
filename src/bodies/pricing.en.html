How billing works

No surprises, by design

B.1

Per person, honestly

You pay for people who can sign in — not for rows in a table. Disable a member and their seat frees on the spot; re-enabling simply checks the limit again. Adding seats mid-period bills only the prorated remainder; reducing seats settles at your next renewal — and when a smaller count would cost more, you keep the cheaper one. You subscribe and pay by card inside the app, from Settings › Billing; the payment window is Paddle’s and card details never touch GearLogs.

B.2

Try it properly first

Access is by invitation, and evaluation periods are arranged during onboarding — no payment details needed. Load your real inventory, run a real month, then decide.

B.3

Billed by Paddle

Paddle is our merchant of record: they process payment, apply the correct taxes, and issue your invoices. Card details are entered on Paddle's secure pages and never touch GearLogs.

B.4

Leave without a fight

Cancel yourself, any time — it takes effect at the period end, and one click undoes it before then. Afterwards your workspace stays read-only and fully exportable for 12 months, then deleted with at least 30 days’ notice by email. Your records were always yours; see the refund policy for the rest.
