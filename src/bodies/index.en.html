What it does

One system for the whole store.

From the shelf to the person and back, every step is recorded, and every screen below is the real app.

Logistics

Where is it, and how much is left?

Every item shows its stock, what is in use and what is in repair, group by group, with the activity behind it.

  • In stock, in use, in repair
  • Stock rings per group
  • Every move counted
Stock and capacity

Know what to reorder before you run out.

Storage fill against capacity, the items running low, the shelves over capacity, and 90 days of activity on one dashboard.

  • Fill against capacity
  • A low-stock list
  • Over-capacity alerts
Hand out

One item or a whole kit, in one act.

Pick the person, tick the items, choose the serial units. The numbers are checked before anything moves.

  • Stock checked first
  • Serial units chosen or dealt
  • A receipt opens for the person
Approvals

Every hand‑out opens a receipt to sign.

The person signs for what they received on My Gear, or says what is wrong, with no account and no password. A dispute comes back to you with the reason.

  • Waiting · signed · disputed
  • A reason from a list, and their own words
  • An unsigned receipt expires after 14 days
People

Everything a person ever received, returned or used.

Open anyone and see their whole history: every item received, every return, every consumable dispensed, with the date and who handled it.

  • Received and returned
  • Consumables dispensed
  • Dated, with who handled it
Registry

Every serial unit’s whole life.

Serial numbers, holders, condition and warranty on every unit. In repair, lost and written off are states the whole team can see.

  • Warranty and service contracts
  • In repair · lost · written off
  • An unserviceable unit can’t be issued by mistake
