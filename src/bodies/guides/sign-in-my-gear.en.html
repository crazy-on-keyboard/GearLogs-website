Guide 4.26 · My Gear

Sign a receipt, or say what is wrong

Sign for the gear on your name, or say what is wrong with it. Share this guide with your people: it shows My Gear as they see it.

Before you start

  • Guide 4.25 gives your people their sign-in to My Gear. They sign in with a code sent to their email.
  • A receipt opens by itself for every hand-out, return and write-off on your name. A notice in My Gear says A receipt is waiting for your signature.
  • You have 14 days to sign. A receipt nobody signs expires. Nothing changes on your record, and your administrators are told in the bell.
  • The gear never waits for your signature. It is on your name from the moment the storeman records it.

  1. Open Approvals on My Gear.

    Sign in to My Gear with the code from your email. Then open Approvals in the Sections list.

    1. Approvals in the Sections list. The number beside it counts the receipts waiting for you: 1 waiting.
    2. A waiting receipt: TO SIGN, what happened (Issued to you), its HO code, how many lines it has and how many days are left.

    Receipts you have already decided stay in the list, below the waiting ones.

  2. Open the receipt and read it.

    Click the receipt. It opens beside the list.

    1. The facts: what happened, who recorded it, the day, and how many days you have left to sign. A receipt from a kit names the kit too.
    2. The lines, exactly as they were recorded: item, code, quantity and serial. A consumable says consumed. A write-off adds the reason.
    3. I received these, when every line is right.
    4. Something is wrong, when a line is not. Step 4 covers it.

    The question above the two buttons says who handed the gear over, and when. For a return, the button reads I returned these. For a write-off, it reads That’s right. For a single item, it reads I received it or I returned it.

  3. Confirm your signature.

    I received these asks once more, in the same place.

    1. The sentence says what you sign for, and on which receipt. A signature cannot be undone.
    2. Confirm and sign signs the receipt.
    3. Back returns to the question, with nothing signed.

    Once signed, the receipt reads SIGNED, and the count in the Sections list goes down by one.

  4. Or say what is wrong.

    Something is wrong opens a short form under the lines.

    1. What is wrong? Pick one reason: I did not receive it, Wrong item, Wrong quantity, Damaged or Other.
    2. Which lines? Tick the lines it is about, up to 50. This is optional.
    3. Anything to add? Your own words, up to 400 characters. With Other, they are required.
    4. Read the line above the button, then press Send the dispute.

    Your words go to your workspace’s administrators, and to GearLogs support only while the workspace has opened a support view. A dispute is final for you: a member of your team closes it with you. Your administrators are told in the bell. Back leaves the form with nothing sent.

  5. Find the gear on your name already.

    Open My gear in the Sections list.

    1. My gear in the Sections list, with the number of items on your name.
    2. The gear on your name: item, code, quantity, serial and the day it was recorded.

    The gear does not wait for your signature. It moved to you the moment the storeman recorded it. Here, the Hi-Vis Vest and the Hard Hat are listed while their receipt still waits. The Safety Gloves were consumable, so they are not held.

Result

Your receipt is signed, or your dispute is with your administrators. Either way, the receipt stays in your list with its state, and one sentence under the lines says what was decided and when.

If something is refused

  • This receipt was already decided. Someone decided it first: you, from another device, or a member of your team who confirmed it on your behalf or cancelled it. The receipt loads again and shows its state.
  • This receipt expired before you signed — nothing changed on your record. Its 14 days are over. The receipt now reads EXPIRED, and your administrators are told in the bell.
  • Write what happened — it is required when you pick Other. You picked Other with no words. Write what happened, then send again.
  • Check the words: at most 400 characters, no hidden characters. The words are too long, or a hidden character came in with a paste. Shorten them, or type them again.
  • Your decision could not be recorded. Try again. GearLogs could not record your answer this time. Try again in a moment. If it is refused again, tell your administrator.
