Guide 4.10 · Settings

Manage your subscription and seats

See what your workspace runs on, choose a plan and pay by card, then add seats and extras as your team grows.

Before you start

  • Settings › Billing opens for administrators only.
  • There is one plan, and every feature is in the price. It includes five accounts. Each seat beyond them costs the same flat amount.
  • A seat is a member who can sign in. Disabling a login frees its seat at once.
  • The amounts on the screen are Paddle’s live prices. You pay in Paddle’s secure window, so card details never touch GearLogs.

  1. Open Settings › Billing.

    Open Settings from the side menu, then Billing in its list.

    1. Settings in the side menu.
    2. Billing in the Settings list. Only administrators see it.
    3. The status card: what the workspace runs on, and how many seats are in use.

    What stands below the card depends on the state: the plan to choose, or the boxes of a running subscription.

  2. Read where your workspace stands.

    The status card says what the workspace runs on and how many seats are in use.

    1. The state: ACTIVE for a paid subscription, or the word for what the workspace runs on now, such as LICENSED.
    2. Billing log opens every billing event. Refresh, beside it, reads the status again. Step 5 covers the log.
    3. The seats in use, out of the seats you hold.
    4. No subscription yet stands here until you subscribe.

    With a subscription, the renewal day follows the seats. A failed payment turns the state into PAYMENT PROBLEM, with Update payment method under it.

  3. Choose a plan and subscribe.

    Under Choose your plan, pick the cycle and read the price before you pay.

    1. Monthly or Yearly. The line beside it says what yearly saves: two months free.
    2. The plan’s price, and the five accounts it includes. Every feature is in it.
    3. How many people can sign in today, and what you add after subscribing.
    4. Subscribe — pay by card. Paddle opens a secure payment window over the page.

    Nothing is charged until you confirm inside Paddle’s window. When the payment is confirmed, the screen says Payment received — activating your subscription…, then Your subscription is active.

  4. Add seats and extras after subscribing.

    The (i) beside Subscription lists what a subscription adds to this screen.

    1. The (i) beside Subscription.
    2. What you can do: with a subscription, one box each for expansion seats, Priority Operations, e-mail packs, the cycle, payment and cancellation.
    3. How it works: one plan, a flat price per seat beyond the included accounts, and one optional add-on.

    With a subscription, change one box at a time and press Review change. Paddle’s exact amount appears. Press Confirm, or Back. Adding bills now, pro-rata. Removing takes effect now, and the credit appears on your next invoice. Priority Operations promises a first reply within 4 business hours, Sunday to Thursday, 09:00–18:00 Israel time. Each e-mail pack adds 1,000 e-mails a month.

  5. Read the billing log.

    Billing log on the status card opens every billing action and every confirmation from Paddle.

    1. Back to Billing returns to the card.
    2. The search, Source and the two dates narrow the list.
    3. Reset filters, then CSV, Excel and Print, which export the list as you filtered it.
    4. The log: when, who, the type, the action and the amount.

    Refunds and credits have their own lines: requested, issued, declined or reversed. A refund waits for Paddle’s approval before it is paid. Invoices are listed under Payment & invoices, and PDF opens each one on Paddle’s page.

Result

Your workspace runs on the plan you chose, with every feature included. Every change appears in the billing log once Paddle confirms it, and the screen says Change confirmed. To stop, Cancel at period end… keeps full access until the paid period ends, and Resume subscription undoes it until then. After that the workspace turns read-only; your data stays visible and exportable for 12 months.

If something is refused

  • Unable to add a member — every seat this workspace holds is in use. Every seat is taken. Add expansion seats in Settings › Billing, or disable a login that no longer needs access.
  • Cannot re-enable this user — all seats are in use. Disable another user or raise the seat limit first. Turning a login back on needs a free seat. Disable another login, or add expansion seats first.
  • The subscription can’t change right now (a renewal or payment retry is in progress). Try again shortly. Paddle is renewing the subscription or retrying a payment. Wait a moment, then press Review change again.
  • Live prices are unavailable right now — refresh the page or try again in a minute. Paddle’s prices did not arrive, so Subscribe — pay by card stays grey. Refresh the page in a minute.
  • The payment window couldn’t load. Check your connection or an ad blocker, then try again. Paddle’s window did not open. An ad blocker often stops it. Allow the page, then press Subscribe — pay by card again.
  • A cancellation is already scheduled. The subscription is already set to end with the period. Resume subscription undoes it.
  • Your last payment didn’t go through Paddle retries automatically for up to 30 days, and you are told by e-mail too. Update payment method, under the message, usually fixes it on the next retry.
