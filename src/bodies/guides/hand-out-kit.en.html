Guide 4.22 · Kits

Hand out a kit

Make a kit once, with its items and how many of each. Then hand it to a whole group, a tab or a few people in one go.

Before you start

  • A kit is a named list of items, with how many of each go in one kit. Its code starts with KT-.
  • ISSUE is for anyone who holds the right Assign/Return Assets. To build or change a kit you need Manage kits.
  • If the kit has a sensitive item, you also need Assign Sensitive Gear.

  1. Open the kits.

    1. Open Logistics from the side menu.
    2. Switch the area to Kits. This browser remembers your choice.
    3. ISSUE hands this kit out. It shows only on a kit that has at least one line.
    4. The kit’s lines: each item, and how many go in one kit. ISSUED TO counts the people who have received the kit.

  2. Build a kit, or change one.

    This needs Manage kits.

    1. + KIT makes a new kit.
    2. LINE adds an item to the kit, with how many go in one kit.
    3. A line can be a plain item, a consumable or a serialized item. The Type column says which.

    An item can be on a kit only once, and an archived item cannot join a kit.

  3. Press ISSUE, and pick who receives it.

    Issue kit opens with two panes: Who receives it and What will happen.

    1. The pane counts the people on the list.
    2. Search for a person, a group or a tab. The search narrows the rows, and your ticks stay.
    3. Tick a group to take everyone in it. A tick on a tab takes the whole tab.
    4. Untick one name to leave that person out.

    The list is fixed when you press Issue. Someone added to the group later is not included.

  4. Check what will happen, then issue.

    1. How many people are selected.
    2. Every line: how many each person gets, how many the whole list needs, and how many are in stock.
    3. How many people will get a notice. Only people linked to a login get one.
    4. The green button says how many people will receive the kit. Press it to issue.

    On a serialized line, the pane also shows which unit each person gets. The oldest unit in stock comes first. Change picks another unit for that person.

  5. If a line is short.

    The stock is checked for the whole list at once.

    1. Here the whole Crew tab is ticked.
    2. A line turns red when Needed is more than In stock. If you press Issue now, nothing is issued, and the refusal names every short line.

    Take people off the list, or add stock, until no line is red.

  6. See the kit on the person’s card.

    Each person holds the kit as one row, at the top of the gear they hold.

    1. The kit’s name and code, the day it was issued, how many lines are still out, and the next date promised back.
    2. Return takes the kit back from this person.
    3. Open the row to see each item and its state. Consumables are not listed, because they do not come back.

    The row keeps the kit’s name and lines as they were issued, even if the kit changes later. Each person also gets a hand-over receipt to sign, as with any hand-out.

Result

Everyone on the list holds the kit’s lines, the stock goes down by exactly what went out, and the kit card counts who received it. On Analytics, the Kits source gives three cards: Kit lines out, Kit lines out, by person and Kits issued vs returned. Under Data Ops › Exports, Logistics Reports holds Kit Contents and Kit Issues & Returns.

If something is refused

  • Not enough stock for one line — take people off the list or add stock A red line is still short. When more lines are short, the sentence counts them, and the short lines are listed under it.
  • Pick at least one person Nobody is ticked.
  • At most 300 people in one issue — split the list One issue takes at most 300 people.
  • This item is sensitive — you need the Assign Sensitive Gear permission to hand it out. The kit holds a sensitive item, and you do not hold that right.
  • … is archived — remove the line from the kit first An item on the kit was archived. The sentence starts with the item’s code and name.
  • Today’s sending limit reached Issue asks first: the people on the list get their notice in the app, not by email, until tomorrow. Issue with in-app notices goes ahead.
