Guide 4.13 · Search

Find anything from the search box

Type a few letters at the top of any screen to reach an item, a person, a unit or a record, and see why each result matched.

Before you start

  • The search box sits at the top of every screen and takes up to 80 characters.
  • It finds items, people, departments, groups, units, lots and service contracts.
  • The Users group appears only for administrators.
  • Archived records appear only with the right Write Off / Report Lost Gear. Administrators and operators have it.
  • A person’s e-mail, phone, notes, identity number and personal number are searched only with the right View Contact Details. Administrators and operators have it.

  1. Type a word in the search box.

    Type part of a name, a code or a serial number. The results open under the box as you type.

    1. The search box, at the top of every screen.
    2. Items: the results come grouped by type, each type under its own heading.
    3. The best match comes first. The letters you typed are highlighted.
    4. Units, then Lots, follow below. Scroll the list to see them all.

    The board behind the box thins to the matching items or people. If this tab holds none of them, the board opens the tab that does. Each group shows up to five results.

  2. Open a result.

    Click a result to go straight to it. An item or a person opens in a window over the board.

    1. The item’s Stock, Location, Group and Status.
    2. Assigned To: who holds the item, and how many.

    Other results open their own screen. A unit or a lot opens the Registry on its item. A department or a group opens its tab on the board. A service contract opens Contracts in the Registry. An archived record opens the Archive, and a user opens Users.

  3. See why a result matched.

    A result whose name does not hold your word says where it matched.

    1. Matched on names the field and shows the text, such as Matched on Manufacturer — Vantix Scan.
    2. Show all ends a group that has more than five matches, and says how many there are. Under Departments and Groups it opens the rest right there in the list; under the other groups it opens that kind’s own screen.

    The search reads names and reference codes, serial and lot numbers, locations, groups and departments, designations, the manufacturer, model, category, SKU and GTIN, and the notes you wrote. A unit is also found by its item’s name. A service contract is found by its provider and contract number.

  4. Take the search to its screen.

    Show all opens the screen for that kind of result, with your search already in place. Here it opened the Registry from the Units group.

    1. The Registry’s filter box, holding your word.
    2. Items: only the items that match.
    3. Units: their units, each with its code, serial number, status and holder.

    Items and people keep the search on the Logistics and Personnel boards. Service contracts take it to Contracts in the Registry. Users and archived records open their full list, without the search. Choosing another screen from the side menu, or another language, clears the box.

Result

From any screen, a few letters take you to the item, person, unit or record you need.

If something is refused

  • Nothing matched No record you can see holds those letters. The line under it lists what the search covers for you. Check the spelling, or try part of the word or a code.
  • That result is no longer available — it may have just been deleted. The department or group you clicked was removed a moment ago. Search again.
