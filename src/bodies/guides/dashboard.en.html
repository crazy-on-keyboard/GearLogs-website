Guide 4.4 · Dashboard

Build your dashboard, and arrange it

Add ready-made widgets or build your own, choose who sees each one, and arrange them on the grid.

Before you start

  • The dashboard is Analytics in the side menu. It opens for anyone who holds the right View everyone’s analytics (the dashboard’s numbers) or View Own Analytics.
  • Adding, editing and arranging widgets needs the right Manage Tabs & Groups. Administrators and operators have it.
  • With View Own Analytics alone, the activity widgets count only your own actions. Three of the four Approvals widgets count the receipts you recorded, captioned Receipts you recorded. Waiting for a signature is not available to you.

  1. Open Analytics.

    1. Open Analytics from the side menu.
    2. The time range: 1 Month, 3 Months, 6 Months, 1 Year or All Time. The dashboard opens on 3 Months.
    3. Edit Layout arranges the widgets.
    4. Add Widget opens the Widget Builder.

    A widget that depends on time names its window at its foot, such as 3 Months · Week.

  2. Pick a ready-made widget.

    Add Widget opens on Ready-made widgets.

    1. Filter the gallery by source: All, or one of the eight sources.
    2. The ready-made widgets. Pick one.
    3. Build your own starts from a blank widget: see step 4.

    Among them: Activity by type, Stock by Location, Capacity fill, Kit lines out, Receipts by state, Waiting for a signature and Loss by Reason.

  3. Name it, size it, choose who sees it.

    Picking a widget opens Finish. The Preview at the top draws it with your real data.

    1. Widget Title: the field shows the widget’s own name. Leave it empty to keep that name, or type your own, up to 80 characters.
    2. Size on Dashboard: up to 4 columns wide and 2 rows tall.
    3. Who sees it: Everyone, Administrators or Only me. Only an administrator is offered Administrators.
    4. Create Widget adds it. The dashboard switches to arranging, so you can place it.

    A widget for administrators wears ADMINS ONLY, and a private one ONLY ME. Nobody else sees an Only me widget, and only you can edit or remove it.

  4. Build your own: ask a question.

    Build your own opens Source. Pick what to count: Inventory Items, Personnel, Activity Logs, Write-offs, Registry, Kits, Approvals or Board shortcuts. Then Next.

    1. Preview: the widget as it will look. It redraws with every choice.
    2. The four steps: Source, Question, Visual, Finish. A finished step takes you back to it.
    3. Filter By, then Value matches: count only what matches.
    4. Group By: split the count into groups, such as by Action type.

    Question appears only for Inventory Items, Personnel, Activity Logs and Kits. The other sources go straight to Visual.

  5. Pick a visual, and its period.

    1. The visuals this source can show. One that needs a group is grey and says (Requires Group By).
    2. Period: Dashboard period follows the time range on the band. Own period keeps a window of its own.
    3. How far back: 7, 14, 30, 60, 90, 180 or 365 days, or All time.
    4. Group time by: Day, Week or Month, for trend charts.

    Ranked charts and lists also offer Show top: 3, 5, 12 or 25, and a list can show All. Stock, people and Registry widgets show today’s numbers: the builder says Not affected by the dashboard period. Next leads to Finish, as in step 3.

  6. Arrange the dashboard.

    Edit Layout turns on arranging. Every widget shows its tools.

    1. Done ends arranging.
    2. Move Backward and Move Forward move the widget one place.
    3. The size button, 1×1 here, opens the size grid. A click resizes the widget at once.
    4. Edit reopens the Widget Builder. Remove asks before it removes the widget.

    Nothing needs saving: each change is kept as you make it. System Status, shown to administrators, cannot be moved, resized or removed.

Result

Your dashboard holds the widgets your team needs, each sized and placed, and each seen only by the people it is meant for.

If something is refused

  • Pick a group-by first — this visual needs one. The visual you chose needs Group By. Go back to Question and pick one. Next stays grey until then.
  • Pick a value to match, or set Filter By back to None. Filter By is set, but Value matches is empty. Next stays grey until you pick a value.
  • The widget could not be saved — nothing changed. Try again. The workspace refused the save, for example when the connection dropped or your rights changed. Nothing was added.
  • Not available to your role. A write-off widget, seen by someone without Write Off / Report Lost Gear. The losses stay with the people who can open the Archive.
  • This list shows everyone’s receipts, not only the ones you recorded — so it is not available on your dashboard. Waiting for a signature, seen by someone who holds only View Own Analytics.
