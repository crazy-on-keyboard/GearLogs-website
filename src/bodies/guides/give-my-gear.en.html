Guide 4.25 · Personnel

Give your people My Gear

Put an email and a number on a person’s record, and they can sign in to My Gear to see their own gear, notices and receipts.

Before you start

  • You need the rights Create/Edit Personnel and View Contact Details. Administrators and operators have both.
  • Without View Contact Details, the email and the two numbers are not on the form.
  • A person signs in to My Gear with three things: your workspace code, their ID number or personal number, and the email on their record.
  • Your workspace code looks like TN-001. It is at the top of the Personnel screen, with a Copy button and an (i) — every member sees it there.

  1. Find who cannot sign in yet.

    On Personnel, one filter lists everyone who cannot sign in to My Gear yet.

    1. Open Personnel from the side menu.
    2. Cannot receive a code: press it to list only them. The number is how many there are.
    3. No code: the mark on each of them. They are missing an email, or both numbers.
    4. Edit Record opens the person’s details.

    The filter only narrows the board. Press it again to see everyone.

  2. Fill in the email and a number.

    In Edit Person, the sign-in details sit with the contact details.

    1. Email: the address the sign-in code goes to. Without it, no code can reach the person.
    2. ID number: one of the two numbers a person can sign in with.
    3. Personal number: the other one. Either number is enough.
    4. Save.

    Type a number as the person knows it. Spaces, dashes and other signs do not count, and neither do capital letters. No two people in your workspace can share a number. Leave both numbers empty for someone who will not sign in.

  3. Check the person’s card.

    Open the card with Show details. Its contact line shows what you saved.

    1. The email on record.
    2. ID number: a dash means there is none.
    3. Personal number.

    Once the email and a number are there, the No code mark clears. The Personnel Roster export, under Data Ops › Exports, carries both numbers with the contact details, for anyone who holds View Contact Details. To fill many people at once, the personnel import takes an email, an ID number and a personal number.

  4. Tell them how to open My Gear.

    Give each person your workspace code — copy it from the top of the Personnel screen — and the address my.gearlogs.com. They type the code, their ID number or personal number and their email. A sign-in code then reaches that email. Once inside, they see four sections:

    1. My gear: everything they hold.
    2. Notices: gear handed to them, a return recorded, an item on their name written off, a receipt to sign.
    3. History: what was handed to them, used up or written off, and every move of a serial-numbered unit or a kit line. A plain item they returned keeps its hand-out line, with each return listed.
    4. Approvals: the receipts waiting for their signature.

    With Morning mail to your people on, in Settings › Notifications, a morning email reaches them when something new waits. Its link fills in the workspace code for them.

  5. Change or end their access.

    The same Edit Person window keeps My Gear in step with the record.

    1. Email: change it, and My Gear signs the person out. They sign in again with the new address.
    2. Archive: My Gear signs the person out and no longer lets them in. Archive is refused while they still hold gear.

    An archived person receives no more notices.

Result

Your people sign in to My Gear with their own details. They see what they hold and sign their receipts, and their access follows the record.

If something is refused

  • This ID number is already on another person in this workspace Another record already holds this number. A number belongs to one person only.
  • This personal number is already on another person in this workspace The same rule for the personal number.
  • Enter at least one letter or digit A number made only of spaces or signs is refused.
  • Enter an email like name@company.com The address is not in the shape of an email. Check it for a missing @ or domain.
  • Person is holding assigned assets. Archive waits until the person has returned everything. The confirm button stays greyed.
  • Wrong My Gear sign-in codes are being entered for PR-017 A notice in your bell, when wrong codes keep being typed for one person. It names the person by code. Ask the person whether it was them.
  • My Gear sign-in codes: today’s allowance for this workspace is used up — one request was not answered Your workspace sent all the sign-in codes it may send today. No further codes go out until the next day.
