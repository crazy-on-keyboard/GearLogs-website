Guide 4.27 · Approvals

Read a receipt, and act on it

See every receipt your people have to sign, open one to read what happened, and close the ones that need you.

Before you start

  • Everyone in the workspace can open Approvals and read the receipts of the people they can see. Acting on a receipt, and reading its notes, needs the right Confirm a hand-over on behalf. Administrators have it.
  • A receipt opens by itself for every hand-out, return and write-off to a person. The person signs it on My Gear within 14 days.

  1. Open Approvals.

    1. Open Approvals from the side menu.
    2. The rail counts every state: Waiting, Disputed, Signed, On behalf, Cancelled and Expired. The counts update live.

  2. Open a receipt to read it.

    1. Pick a receipt in the list. Its facts open at the top: the act, the person, who recorded it and when.
    2. Its lines, exactly as they were handed over. A line the person disputed says named in the dispute.
    3. The timeline: opened, the morning mail queued, sent, delivered or not, disputed with the person’s reason, signed, confirmed on behalf or cancelled. Every entry names who did it.
    4. Show notes opens the words behind a dispute or a decision, for the same people who can act on the receipt.

  3. Act on a receipt that is still open.

    1. Confirm on behalf closes it as received.
    2. Resend reminder sends the person a reminder now, once per person a day. During quiet hours it is refused and says when they end.
    3. Cancel receipt closes one recorded in error.

    Confirming and cancelling both need a reason of at least 8 characters. The reason stays with the receipt: it is never mailed or logged.

  4. Spot open receipts on the board.

    A receipt’s state also shows as a small mark on the board. The mark opens the receipt.

    1. On the person’s card.
    2. On a kit’s row. The item’s card carries the same mark.

  5. Fix a person who cannot receive a code.

    Someone with no email on record, or with neither an ID number nor a personal number, cannot sign in to My Gear.

    1. On Personnel, the Cannot receive a code filter lists them all, with their count.
    2. Each of them carries the No code mark.

    Add the missing detail on the person’s card, and the mark clears.

  6. Follow receipts on the dashboard and in exports.

    The Approvals source gives four dashboard cards:

    1. Receipts by state: how many are in each state.
    2. Waiting for a signature: who is waiting, and the longest wait.
    3. Time to confirm: the average time to a confirmation.
    4. Opened vs confirmed: receipts opened and confirmed over time.

    Under Data Ops › Exports, Approvals Reports holds Hand-over receipts and Receipt lines. The email column needs View Contact Details, and the notes never leave the app.

Result

You see every receipt by state, you close the open ones that need you, and you follow them on the dashboard.

If something is refused

  • This receipt expired unsigned; the gear may still be out. A receipt past its 14 days cannot be confirmed.
  • This receipt is closed — nothing more to do. It was already signed, confirmed on behalf or cancelled.
  • Resend reminder during quiet hours is refused, and says when the quiet hours end.
