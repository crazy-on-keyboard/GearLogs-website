Guide 4.27 · Approvals

Read a receipt, and act on it

See every receipt your people have to sign, open one to read what happened, and close the ones that need you.

Before you start

  • Approvals is for administrators and for anyone who holds the right Confirm a hand-over on behalf.
  • A receipt opens by itself for every hand-out, return and write-off to a person. The person signs it on My Gear within 14 days.

  1. Open Approvals.

    The rail on the left counts every state: Waiting, Disputed, Signed, On behalf, Cancelled and Expired. The counts update live.

  2. Open a receipt to read it.

    A receipt shows its facts, its lines exactly as they were handed over, and a timeline: opened, the morning mail queued, sent, delivered or not, disputed with the person’s reason, signed, confirmed on behalf or cancelled. Every entry names who did it.

    Show notes opens the words behind a dispute or a decision, for the same people who can act on the receipt.

  3. Act on a receipt that is still open.

    1. Confirm on behalf closes it as received.
    2. Resend reminder sends the person a reminder now, once per person a day. During quiet hours it is refused and says when they end.
    3. Cancel receipt closes one recorded in error.

    Confirming and cancelling both need a reason of at least 8 characters. The reason stays with the receipt: it is never mailed or logged.

  4. Spot open receipts on the board.

    The same state shows as a small mark on the person’s card, the item’s card and a kit’s row. The mark opens the receipt.

  5. Fix a person who cannot receive a code.

    Someone with no email on record, or with neither an ID number nor a personal number, cannot sign in to My Gear. They carry a No code mark on Personnel, where a filter lists them all.

    Add the missing detail on the person’s card, and the mark clears.

  6. Follow receipts on the dashboard and in exports.

    The Approvals source gives four dashboard cards: Receipts by state, Waiting for a signature, Time to confirm and Opened vs confirmed.

    Under Data Ops › Exports, Approvals Reports holds Hand-over receipts and Receipt lines. The email column needs View contact details, and the notes never leave the app.

Result

You see every receipt by state, you close the open ones that need you, and you follow them on the dashboard.

If something is refused

  • This receipt expired unsigned; the gear may still be out. A receipt past its 14 days cannot be confirmed.
  • This receipt is closed — nothing more to do. It was already signed, confirmed on behalf or cancelled.
  • Resend reminder during quiet hours is refused, and says when the quiet hours end.
