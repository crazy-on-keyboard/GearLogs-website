Guide 4.1 · Board

Build your board from tabs and groups

Give each department its own tab, add groups inside it, fill them with your items or people, and arrange the board in the order you read it.

Before you start

  • Adding and editing tabs and groups, and Edit Layout, need the right Manage Tabs & Groups. Administrators and operators have it.
  • Adding items to a group, and dragging them, need Create/Edit Items. On Personnel, people need Create/Edit Personnel. Administrators and operators have both.
  • Only a Super Administrator can delete a tab or a group, and only an empty one.
  • The Logistics and Personnel boards work the same way. This guide shows Logistics.

  1. Open the board on Logistics.

    Open Logistics from the side menu, on Equipment. The board opens on its first tab.

    1. Logistics in the side menu.
    2. The open tab, filled with its own colour. Inside it sit View activity log and Edit.
    3. + TAB adds a tab at the end of the row.

    Each tab is a department. Tabs wrap onto a new row as you add more.

  2. Name the tab and pick its colour.

    + TAB opens a window of the same name.

    1. Tab Name: up to 30 characters. No two tabs in the workspace share a name.
    2. Color Indicator: the tab’s outline, and its fill while it is open.
    3. Save adds the tab and opens it.

    GearLogs gives every tab its own code, such as TB-001. It shows in Edit Tab, and it cannot be changed.

  3. Add groups to the tab.

    The dashed + GROUP box after the last group opens the + GROUP window.

    1. Group Name: up to 40 characters. No two groups in the same tab share a name.
    2. Color Indicator: the border of the group’s card.
    3. Notes: up to 200 characters, shown at the top of the card.
    4. Save adds the group at the end of the tab.

    Each group gets its own code, such as GR-001, shown under its name.

  4. Fill a group.

    Each group card carries its own buttons in its header.

    1. The group’s name, its (i) and its code.
    2. View activity log, and the ⋮ menu with Edit. For a Super Administrator it also holds Delete.
    3. Add opens + ITEM for a new item in this group. On Personnel it opens + PERSON.

    Every item and every person sits in one group. To move a card, drag it onto another group, or onto a tab to put it in that tab’s first group. Move this? asks first, and nothing moves until you press Move.

  5. Read a group card on Logistics.

    Above its list, every Logistics group card sums up its gear.

    1. Stock: a ring of what is In stock, In use and In repair.
    2. Activity: the group’s moves over the last 30, 60 or 90 days, in columns. Your choice applies to every card.
    3. In use by department: who holds the group’s gear now, by department.
    4. The group’s items, each on its own card.

    The summary always describes the whole group, even while a search narrows the board. Group cards on Personnel have no summary.

  6. Arrange the groups.

    Edit Layout at the end of the tab row adds the layout controls to every group card.

    1. Done leaves the mode.
    2. Move Backward and Move Forward move a group earlier or later in the reading order.
    3. Width: how many columns the group spans.

    Each press saves at once, for everyone. Groups take turns across the columns and stack down each one, so a short group never leaves a gap under it. How many groups sit across a row is your own setting: Settings › Display › My Display › Groups Per Row, from 1 to 6. A group never spans more columns than that.

  7. Rename, recolour or move a tab.

    Edit on the open tab opens Edit Tab. A right-click on a tab offers it too.

    1. ID Code: the tab’s own code.
    2. Tab Name.
    3. Color Indicator.
    4. Position: Move Backward and Move Forward move the tab one place along the row, at once.

    Save keeps a new name or colour. A move needs no Save.

Result

Every department has its own tab, every group its own colour and code, and the board reads in the order you set.

If something is refused

  • Required The name is empty. Type a name, then press Save.
  • Name already exists. Another tab in the workspace, or another group in this tab, already has that name. Pick another.
  • Target department has no groups to receive the item/person. You dropped a card on a tab that has no groups. Add a group to that tab first.
  • This tab still has groups inside it. Empty it first, then delete. A Super Administrator tried to delete a tab that still holds groups. Move or delete its groups first.
  • This group still has items or people inside it. Empty it first, then delete. The group still holds cards. Move them to another group first.
