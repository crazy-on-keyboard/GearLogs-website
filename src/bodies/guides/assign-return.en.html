Guide 4.2 · Hand-outs and returns

Hand out gear, and take it back

Hand gear to one person or to many, and take it back when it comes in. The stock follows every step.

Before you start

  • Handing gear out and taking it back needs the right Assign/Return Assets. Without it, the buttons are dimmed, and a click says why.
  • A sensitive item also needs the right Assign Sensitive Gear.
  • Every hand-out and every return opens a hand-over receipt. The person signs it on My Gear.
  • A consumable is dispensed, not lent. It never comes back, so it has no Return.

  1. Hand an item to one person.

    1. On the item’s card on the board, press the green Assign Item.
    2. Select person… opens a list you can search by name, code, department, email or phone.
    3. Set the Quantity. It never goes above what is in stock.
    4. OK hands it out. The stock drops at once.

  2. Or start from the person.

    On Personnel, the same button sits on the person’s card.

    1. Assign Item on the person’s card.
    2. Select Item... lists every item in stock, with its stock beside the name. Search it by code, name or location.
    3. Set the Quantity.
    4. OK hands it out.

    Serial-numbered items are not in this list. Hand them out from the item’s card (step 4), or with ↗ More items, which opens the hand-out window for this person.

  3. Hand one item to several people at once.

    ↗ More people on the item’s Assign card opens the hand-out window, with what you already typed filled in.

    1. Under Who receives it, tick a whole group, a whole tab or single people.
    2. Give each person their own quantity.
    3. After shows what each person will hold. The total line shows what is left in stock.
    4. The button counts the people on the list. One press hands it out to all of them.

    A list that asks for more than the stock turns red and says how many are short. Nothing is handed out until the list fits. ↗ More items on a person’s card opens the same window the other way round: one person, many items.

  4. Hand out a serial-numbered unit.

    On a serial-numbered item’s card, Assign Item opens Assign Unit.

    1. Pick a unit… lists the units in stock. A unit that needs repair carries its mark, and asks again before it goes out.
    2. Select person… picks who gets it.
    3. ↗ More people hands units to several people in the window. It deals the oldest serviceable units first, and never deals a unit that needs repair by itself.
    4. Assign hands out the unit.

    One hand-out deals up to 1,000 serial-numbered units. Split a bigger one into smaller hand-outs.

  5. Find what a person holds.

    On Personnel, open the person’s card.

    1. Assigned Items counts everything the person holds, kits included.
    2. Each held item has its own Return button.

    A kit comes back as a whole kit. Guide 4.23 shows how.

  6. Take it back.

    Return opens Return Item.

    1. The dialog names the item and how many the person holds.
    2. Quantity to Return starts at everything they hold. Lower it to take back only part.
    3. ALL puts it back to everything.
    4. Confirm Return puts the gear back in stock.

    A serial-numbered unit always comes back as one whole unit.

Result

The gear shows on the person’s card, the stock has moved, and the person has a hand-over receipt to sign on My Gear. Every hand-out and every return is kept in the history.

If something is refused

  • Denied: Assign Assets You do not hold the right to hand out or take back gear. The button stays dimmed.
  • This item is sensitive — you need the Assign Sensitive Gear permission to hand it out. The item is sensitive and you lack the right. In the hand-out window, such an item stays in the list, greyed, with an (i) that says why.
  • Not enough in stock. The stock changed while you were choosing. The board refreshes; pick a smaller quantity.
  • That item or person is no longer available. The person or the item was archived. In the hand-out window the same cause reads: “Someone on the list or the item is no longer available — the list was refreshed”.
  • Lower the quantities or take people off the list In the hand-out window: the list asks for more than the stock. The banner says how many are needed and how many are in stock, and nothing is handed out.
  • No items are in stock to issue. A serial-numbered item with no unit in stock. Nothing opens.
  • At most 1,000 serial-numbered units in one hand-out — split it into smaller hand-outs One hand-out asks for more than 1,000 serial-numbered units. Split it.
