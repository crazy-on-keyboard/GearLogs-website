Guide 4.7 · Write-offs

Write off lost gear, and retire a leaver

Record gear that is lost, stolen or broken, retire someone who left with gear in one step, and find every loss in the Archive.

Before you start

  • Writing off needs the right Write Off / Report Lost Gear. Administrators and operators have it.
  • Archiving a person also needs Delete Personnel. Administrators have it.
  • The Archive opens only for people with the write-off right. Search follows the same rule.
  • This guide covers gear a person holds. Shelf stock that nobody holds is written off from the item itself: Edit Record, the Archive tab, Write off stock. Only administrators see it.

  1. Open the person’s card.

    Every write-off from a person starts on their card on the Personnel board. Open it with its arrow.

    1. The red Write off button on an item’s row writes off that one item.
    2. Write off items writes off several of their items at once.
    3. Archive retires the person. If they still hold gear, it offers to write it all off first.

    A kit’s lines and serial-numbered units have no row button: pick them in Write off items.

  2. Write off one item.

    The row’s Write off opens Write Off Item.

    1. Reason: Lost, Stolen, Damaged, Destroyed, Worn out, Left with it (unreturned) or Other.
    2. Quantity: how many of what they hold. ALL takes everything they hold.
    3. Date it happened: today, or an earlier day. Never a future day.
    4. Note (optional): what happened. Other needs a note.

    Write Off records it. The item leaves the person, the item’s total drops, and the loss stays in the ledger. The person gets a receipt for it to sign on My Gear.

  3. Write off several items at once.

    Write off items keeps the person active: only the items you pick leave them.

    1. Select all ticks every line.
    2. Or tick the lines one by one, and set how many of each.
    3. One Reason for them all.
    4. Write off, with the number of lines, records them together.

    The date and the note work as for one item. The date cannot be later than today, or earlier than the day the item was handed out. If one line is refused, none is written off. Lines from a kit sit under the kit’s name.

  4. Retire someone who left with gear.

    Archive on a person who still holds gear opens Write off everything & archive.

    1. Items to be written off: everything they still hold, kits included.
    2. Reason starts at Left with it (unreturned). It applies to every item.
    3. Type ARCHIVE to confirm.
    4. Write off all & archive writes everything off and archives the person in one step.

    No receipt is opened: the person has left. For someone who holds nothing, Archive asks a plain Archive this? instead.

  5. Find every write-off in the Archive.

    1. Open Archive, then Write-offs.
    2. Filter by Reason and Status, or search by item or person.
    3. The totals follow the filters: Records shown, Units outstanding and Units recovered.
    4. Recover brings back gear that turned up. It returns to available stock.

    Each row shows the date, the item, the person, who reported it and its status: Active loss, Partial, Recovered or Voided. A write-off of shelf stock says Storeroom. An administrator can Void a write-off made in error, while the person is still active and nothing was recovered: the item goes back to them, and the record stays, marked Voided. The ledger itself is never edited.

  6. Bring a person back.

    1. Personnel Archives lists everyone who was archived.
    2. Filter by On exit: people with write-offs, or a clean exit.
    3. On exit says clean exit, or how many were written off to them.
    4. Restore brings the person back to the board, as they were.

    Their write-offs stay in the ledger. Write-offs on the row shows them, and History opens their activity log.

  7. See losses on the cards and the dashboard.

    A red mark on a card means something written off there is still lost.

    1. The red mark on the person’s card: how many are still lost.
    2. Written Off on the open card: each item, how many times, how many are still lost and when last. A row opens its dated entries.

    Item cards carry the same mark and list. On the dashboard, the Write-offs source adds cards such as Loss by Reason, Loss by Team, Most-Lost Items and Write-Offs Over Time. Only people with the write-off right see them.

Result

Lost gear leaves the person who held it with a reason on record, a leaver is archived in one step, and every loss waits in the Archive until it is recovered.

If something is refused

  • A note is required for the Other reason. The reason Other needs a note. The confirm stays grey until you add one.
  • You can’t write off more than is held. Someone returned part of it meanwhile. The window stays open with the new amount.
  • The date can’t be in the future or before the item was assigned. In Write off items, the date must fall between the day the item was handed out and today.
  • Person is holding assigned assets. Please return all assets before archiving. You pressed Archive for someone who still holds gear, without the write-off right. Return the gear first, or ask someone who can write it off.
