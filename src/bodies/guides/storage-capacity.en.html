Guide 4.17 · Logistics

Set a storage capacity, and see how much room is left

Give an item the most you can hold of it, read its meter on the board, and find the items that are over.

Before you start

  • Setting a capacity needs the right Create/Edit Items. Administrators and operators have it. A locked item also needs Lock/Unlock Item Stock.
  • The capacity counts everything you hold of the item: in stock, in use and in repair together. Gear that is written off leaves the count.
  • Data Ops is for administrators only.

  1. Set the capacity in the item’s form.

    On the Logistics board, open the item with Edit Record, then its Advanced tab.

    1. The Advanced tab of Edit Item.
    2. Storage capacity: the most of this item you can hold, up to 999,999,999. Leave it empty for no capacity.
    3. The preview: the same meter the board will show, with what is Held and the Headroom left.
    4. Save sets it.

    Lowering the capacity under what the item already holds asks first. The switches at the top of this tab act at once, without Save. A spreadsheet import can set capacities too.

  2. Read the meter on the board.

    Every item with a capacity shows a small meter beside its STOCK badge. 0 is at the bottom, the capacity at the top.

    1. STOCK: what is in stock now.
    2. The meter: green is in stock, blue in use, olive in repair.
    3. IN USE: what is out with people.

    On a consumable item, which has no IN USE badge, the meter stands before STOCK. The (i) on the group’s header explains the meter too.

  3. See when an item is over.

    An item is over only when what it holds passes the capacity. Open its Advanced tab the same way.

    1. Storage capacity: 57 for the Shelving Bay.
    2. The preview: the meter’s top cell turns red and a thin line marks the capacity. Beside it, Held 60 / 57, and Over by 3 in red.

    On the board, the same item’s meter is red at the top. Order less, or raise the capacity. Administrators hear the moment an item goes over, once each time it crosses the line: in the app by default, and by email if they choose it in Settings › Notifications.

  4. Follow capacity on the dashboard.

    Open Analytics from the side menu.

    1. Storage fill: the items ranked by how full they are.
    2. Over capacity: only the items past their capacity, the largest overage first.

    The ready-made widgets Capacity fill, Headroom and Over capacity are in Add Widget. Headroom ranks the items by how few more fit. The Full Inventory Audit in Data Ops › Exports lists each item’s Capacity, Held and Headroom.

  5. Find the items that are over.

    Data Ops opens on Overview. Its Data Health card lists every check that finds something.

    1. Open Data Ops from the side menu.
    2. Overview.
    3. Items over their storage capacity. The (i) beside it says what it counts.
    4. How many items are over, and Review, which takes you to the Logistics board.

    The check counts the same way as the meter: in stock, in use and in repair together. When no item is over, the row is gone.

Result

Every item with a capacity shows how full it is: on its card, on the dashboard and in Data Ops. Administrators hear when one goes over.

If something is refused

  • This puts EQ-002 at 31 of 30. Record it anyway? You added stock, registered units or saved the form past the capacity. Nothing is refused: Record anyway records it, and Cancel leaves the item as it was.
  • Read-only — your role can view this record but not change it. Your role does not hold Create/Edit Items, so the window opens as Item details. Ask an administrator.
  • You do not have permission to edit this item. An administrator locked this item. Only someone with Lock/Unlock Item Stock can change it.
