Guide 4.24 · My Gear

Open My Gear

Sign in with a code sent to your email. Then see the gear on your name, your notices and your history.

Before you start

  • My Gear shows only what is yours: your gear, your notices, your history and your receipts.
  • You need three things: your workspace code (it looks like TN-001), your ID number or personal number, and the email on your record.
  • Your administrator gives you the workspace code. If one of the three is missing from your record, ask them to add it.
  • There is no password. Each sign-in uses a code sent to your email.
  • My Gear lives at my.gearlogs.com.

  1. Ask for a code.

    Open my.gearlogs.com and fill in the three fields.

    1. Workspace code: from your administrator. It looks like TN-001.
    2. Your ID number: the ID number or the personal number on your record. Either works.
    3. The e-mail on your record: the address your workspace holds for you.
    4. Send me a code.

    The page always answers with the same sentence: If this record exists, a code is on its way. So nobody can learn from it whether a record exists. The code arrives by email, in your workspace’s language. It has 8 characters. It works for 30 minutes, and only once. The HE button in the corner switches the page to Hebrew on this device.

  2. Sign in with the code.

    Type the code as it is written in the email. Dashes and spaces do not matter. Tick Remember this device for 30 days on your own device only. Then press Sign in. My Gear opens on My gear.

    1. The sections: My gear, Notices, History and Approvals, each with its number.
    2. How many items and units are on your name, and any kit lines still out.
    3. Each row: the item, its code, the quantity, the serial number and the day it was recorded.

    Send a new code works after a minute. A new code cancels the previous one. If the page says it could not reach My Gear right after you asked, ask again: within a minute the code already on its way is kept, and no second email goes out. Without Remember this device for 30 days, the device signs out 12 hours after you sign in; with it ticked, the device stays signed in for 30 days from the sign-in, used or not. You can be signed in on one device at a time: signing in on another device signs this one out. Approvals counts the receipts waiting for your signature. Guide 4.26 shows how to sign one.

  3. Read your notices.

    Pick Notices in the sections.

    1. Notices: how many are new.
    2. A new notice is tinted, with its day and time at the end. Click it to mark it read.

    A notice tells you that gear was handed to you, that your return was recorded, that an item on your name was written off, or that a receipt waits for your signature. A notice you have read stays in the list, with the time you read it.

  4. Look back at your history.

    Pick History in the sections. The newest event comes first.

    1. History: how many events your history holds.
    2. When: the day and time of each event.
    3. What: RECEIVED, DISPENSED for something used up, RETURNED, or WRITTEN OFF with the reason beside it. A write-off that was recovered or voided says RECOVERED or VOIDED.

    A plain item you returned keeps its RECEIVED line, and each return is its own RETURNED line; a line from a kit turns to RETURNED instead. The last column, By, gives the first name of whoever recorded the event, where the record keeps one. A line from a kit carries the KIT mark and the kit’s name. Load more brings the next 50.

  5. Switch the language, or sign out.

    The top bar stays in place on every section.

    1. Your name and your PR code. Check that the page is yours.
    2. HE: the page in Hebrew. The choice stays on this device.
    3. Sign out ends your session. You are signed in on one device at a time, so there is nothing else to end.

    On a shared computer, always sign out when you finish.

Result

You are signed in to My Gear. You see the gear on your name, your notices and your history.

If something is refused

  • Fill in all three fields. One of the three fields is empty.
  • A workspace code looks like TN-001. The code is TN, a dash and digits. Check it with your administrator.
  • That does not look like an e-mail address. Check the address you typed.
  • The code has 8 characters. Type every character from the email.
  • That code is not right or has expired. Check the email, or send a new code. A code works for 30 minutes and only once, and a new code cancels the previous one. Use the newest email, or press Send a new code.
  • One more check is needed from this connection before we can continue. Please press the button once more. A check on your connection needed a second look. Press the button again.
  • We could not reach My Gear. Check your connection and try again. The page could not reach GearLogs, or GearLogs did not answer. Check your connection and press the button again: if you were asking for a code, a press within a minute keeps the code already on its way. If a code arrives after all, use the newest one.
  • Your workspace is on hold. Its administrators are sorting out the account. Your page comes back when they do. Your gear is not shown until then. Sign out still works.
  • You are signed out. Your session ended: you signed out, you signed in on another device, the session ran out of time (12 hours, or 30 days on a remembered device), or an administrator changed the email on your record, archived it, or your workspace was suspended or closed. Press Sign in and ask for a new code.
  • If this record exists, a code is on its way. No code arrives? The page cannot tell you why. Check that the three details match your record, then ask again. After several requests in a short time, wait a while before you ask for another code.
