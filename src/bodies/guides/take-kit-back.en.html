Guide 4.23 · Kits

Take a kit back

See who still holds a kit, and take their lines back in one go: returned, kept out for later, lost or damaged.

Before you start

  • Taking a kit back needs the right Assign/Return Assets. Administrators, operators and users have it.
  • Marking a line Lost or Damaged also needs Write Off / Report Lost Gear. Administrators and operators have it, and so does anyone it was given to.
  • The kit must have been handed out first. See the guide .

  1. Open the people who hold a kit.

    1. On Logistics, switch to Kits.
    2. The OPEN plate counts the kit’s lines still out, across everyone.
    3. The people button opens Who holds this kit. It appears once the kit has been handed out.

  2. Find the person.

    1. Search people, groups or tabs.
    2. The people tree: All people at the top, then each tab and group with its count. Someone who has left the board sits under Not on the board.
    3. One row per hand-out and person: when it was issued, its state, what is still out with the next promised date, and what was returned, lost, damaged or consumed.
    4. Return on a row that still has something out.

  3. Check the lines.

    Return kit opens with that person’s lines on one side, and one decision on the other.

    1. Every line starts as Returned.
    2. What happened: Returned, Later, Lost or Damaged, for the line you picked.
    3. Consumables show CONSUMED. They are used up and do not come back.
    4. The button says what will happen, such as Return 3 items. If everything came back, press it.

    Changed your mind? Return all puts every line back to Returned.

  4. Mark what did not come back.

    Pick the line in the list, then choose what happened to it.

    1. Lost or Damaged writes the line off.
    2. Pick the reason. For Lost: Lost, Stolen, Left with it (unreturned) or Other. For Damaged: Damaged, Destroyed or Worn out.
    3. Date of the loss starts at today and cannot be later. A note is optional, except for Other.
    4. The button now counts both: Return 2 · write off 1.

    Later keeps a line out. It needs the date in Expected back on, and takes an optional note for the storeman. A write-off always asks first: Write off one item? One line is written off to the ledger under your name and leaves the stock for good.

  5. Or start from the person’s card.

    A kit someone holds has its own row on their card.

    1. The kit’s row: its name and code, when it was issued, and how many lines are still out.
    2. Return opens the same Return kit window, for that person’s lines.
    3. Open the row to see every line of the kit, with its state.

Result

The returned lines are back in stock, the losses are in the ledger under your name, and a line kept out waits with its date. The kit’s OPEN plate drops, the Activity Log records the return, and the person’s notice says what happened.

If something is refused

  • 1 field needs fixing A line marked Later with no date, or a loss with the reason Other and no note, still needs a word. The list marks it needs a word, and Go to the first takes you to its field. With more lines the banner counts them.
  • Writing off needs the Write Off / Report Lost Gear right — return the lines and ask an administrator to write off the loss You marked a line Lost or Damaged without the right Write Off / Report Lost Gear.
  • A line was already settled — the list was refreshed Someone settled that line in the meantime. Check the list and save again.
  • … is no longer held by this person — the list was refreshed The item named at the start has left this person since the window opened.
  • Denied: Assign/Return Assets On a person’s card, Return is dimmed without the right Assign/Return Assets, and says so when pressed. In Who holds this kit the button does not show at all.
