Guide 4.6 · History

See who did what, and when

Open the activity log of any person, group, kit or tab, go back as far as you need, and find the history of archived records.

Before you start

  • Anyone can open the history of a person, a group, a kit or a tab.
  • What you see depends on your role. Administrators, and anyone with the right View everyone’s activity log, see every entry. Operators see everyone’s entries except administrators’. Everyone else sees only their own.
  • The Archive opens only for people with the right Write Off / Report Lost Gear. Administrators and operators have it.

  1. Find the history button.

    The history button is a small clock with an arrow around it. Its name is View activity log.

    1. On the tab you are in: the tab’s history, for everyone in it.
    2. On a group’s header: the group’s history, and its people’s.
    3. On a person’s card: that person’s history.

    Logistics has the same buttons on its tabs and groups. There the log covers the items in them: an item has no history button of its own on the board. For the whole workspace at once, open System Logs.

  2. Open a person’s history.

    The button opens the person’s Activity Log, newest first.

    1. The title: the person’s name. The (i) beside it explains the window.
    2. The columns: Timestamp, User, Type and Action. The header stays put as you scroll.
    3. Each row: when, who did it, a badge for the kind of entry, and what happened.

    The badge’s colour follows what happened. A hand-out (ASGN) and a return (RTN) are green, consumables handed out (DISP) amber, a write-off (W-OFF) red. The (i) beside Type names every badge.

  3. Go further back.

    The log opens on the last 30 days.

    1. The count: how many days are shown, and how many entries.
    2. Load 30 more adds 30 days each time.
    3. Load all drops the date limit. It asks first: Load the full history?
    4. Close shuts the window. Escape does the same.

    GearLogs keeps twelve months of activity, up to the newest 50,000 actions per workspace. Older entries are removed.

  4. See a kit’s history.

    1. Open Logistics from the side menu.
    2. Switch to Kits.
    3. The history button on the kit’s card: everything that happened to this kit.

    It opens the same log, with the same count and buttons.

  5. Open the history of an archived record.

    Archived people and items keep their history. Open Archive.

    1. Personnel Archives: History on each person’s row.
    2. Equipment Archives: History on each item’s row.
    3. History opens the same log.

    Every write-off in Write-offs has History too. It opens the item’s log, or the person’s when there is no item. An archived record is often older than 30 days: press Load all to see all of it.

Result

You can see who did what, and when, for any person, group, kit or tab, and for archived records too.

If something is refused

  • No activity yet. Nothing happened in the days shown. Press Load 30 more or Load all. If you only ever see your own entries, your role limits the log.
  • For a busy item or person this can be a lot of entries. Load everything anyway? Load all only asks first. Press Load all again to go on.
