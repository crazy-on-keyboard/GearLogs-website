Guide 4.12 · Reports

Export a report, and back up your workspace

Download or print any of fourteen reports, export a list as you filtered it, and take an encrypted backup that only your password opens.

Before you start

  • Data Ops is for administrators only. Its reports need the right Export Data, and administrators have it.
  • Anyone given Export Data also gets the export buttons on System Logs and on the Archive tabs they can open.
  • A backup needs a Super Administrator.
  • Exporting keeps working even if your subscription lapses.

  1. Open Exports in Data Ops.

    1. Data Ops in the side menu.
    2. Exports on the Data Ops rail.
    3. The reports, grouped by area: Logistics Reports, Personnel Reports, Approvals Reports, Registry Reports, Archive Reports and Activity Reports.

    Fourteen reports in all. Each one reads your complete records from the server, never a clipped list.

  2. Export one report.

    Every row is one report, with three buttons.

    1. The report’s name and what it holds. Here: Full Inventory Audit.
    2. CSV: a plain data file.
    3. Excel: a real Excel workbook.
    4. Print: a clean report page. It is the reliable route to a PDF, Hebrew included.

    CSV and Excel files carry English column headings. A printed page follows the language of the app. When a file is ready, the app says Report downloaded.

  3. Know what the people reports carry.

    1. Personnel Roster: every active person with rank and team. Phone, email, notes, ID number and personal number are added only for someone who holds View Contact Details.
    2. Hand-over receipts: one row per receipt. Who it was for, its state, when it was opened and decided, and by whom. Never the notes. The email column follows the same right.
    3. Receipt lines: one row per frozen line of every receipt.

    Without the right, those columns are simply not there. The report never prints an empty column.

  4. Export straight from a list.

    On System Logs, the same three buttons export what you see.

    1. CSV, Excel and Print at the top of the screen.
    2. The filters: Search Keywords, User and Action Type. The export follows them.
    3. The line at the foot says how many entries the export includes.

    System Logs holds the latest 1,000 entries, and so does its export. For the whole history, export Full Activity Log from Data Ops. The same buttons sit on the Archive tabs — Personnel Archives, Equipment Archives, Contract Archives and Write-offs — and on the billing log in Settings › Billing.

  5. Find the backup in Maintenance.

    1. Maintenance on the Data Ops rail.
    2. Encrypted Backup: Create Backup opens the backup window.
    3. Data Recovery: Open a support request. A backup comes back together with support, never by one click in the app.

    Only a Super Administrator sees Encrypted Backup. The support request opens already filled in with your workspace name and the checklist. Add the backup file and when it was taken.

  6. Take the backup.

    1. Encryption Password: at least 8 characters. It is the only key to the backup.
    2. Cancel closes the window. Nothing is downloaded.
    3. Download Backup stays grey until the password is long enough.

    The encrypted file is saved to your computer. Keep the password apart from it: support will never ask for it by email. The backup holds your records: items, people, hand-outs, write-offs, the Registry, kits and receipts. The activity log and the sign-in accounts are not in it. Nothing in the app changes.

Result

Every report downloads or prints from one screen, a list exports just as you filtered it, and an encrypted backup of your records waits on your own computer.

If something is refused

  • Export failed — please try again. The report could not be read or saved. No file was made. Try again.
  • Backup failed: could not read the full workspace from the server. Nothing was downloaded. The window stays open. Try again in a moment.
  • Backup failed: encryption error Nothing was downloaded, and the window stays open. Try again.
  • Showing the most recent 1,000 entries. Older history is in each person’s or item’s own activity log. System Logs holds only the latest 1,000 entries, and so does its export. For everything, export Full Activity Log from Data Ops.
