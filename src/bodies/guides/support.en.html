Guide 4.16 · Support

Get support inside the app

Ask GearLogs Support a question, report a problem or suggest an idea. Then follow every reply in one place.

Before you start

  • Support opens for every member. Everyone can send a request.
  • Members see their own requests. Administrators can also see every request in the workspace.
  • Each request is a conversation with a person at GearLogs Support.

  1. Open Support.

    Open Support from the side menu. Conversations opens first.

    1. Support in the side menu. A number on it counts the replies you have not opened yet.
    2. Conversations and Notifications. Notifications lists everything the bell has shown you.
    3. New request opens a new conversation with GearLogs Support.
    4. My requests and Workspace: administrators only. Workspace shows every request your team filed.

    Members always see their own requests only. An administrator who opens a teammate’s request can reply on it too.

  2. Say what the request is about.

    Press New request. The window opens on Question.

    1. What is this about?: Question, Problem, Feature idea or Feedback.
    2. Subject: one line, up to 120 characters.
    3. Details: what happened and what you expected. Write at least a few words.
    4. Which item? (optional): shown for a Problem. Pick the item the problem is about.

    A Feature idea asks What do you do today instead? (optional) in its place. As you near a box’s limit, a counter appears under it. It turns amber, then red at the limit. A long paste is never cut.

  3. Check the suggestions, then send.

    As you type the subject, GearLogs looks through your workspace for a match.

    1. Might this help?: items, units, people and contracts in your workspace that match your subject. What’s new in GearLogs opens the site’s changelog.
    2. Attachments: not accepted yet. Describe it in words, or quote the reference codes.
    3. The line beside the buttons says why Send request is greyed.
    4. Send request.

    Suggestions never block sending. Once sent, the window says Received and gives the request its own code, such as SUP-014. Open the conversation takes you to it.

  4. Follow the conversation.

    Your requests wait in the list, sorted by where they stand.

    1. Open, Awaiting your reply and Resolved. Awaiting your reply means Support has answered and waits for you.
    2. Prefer email? A person answers at support@gearlogs.com, separately from these conversations.
    3. Requests: pick one to read it. Each shows its code, subject, type, status and how long ago.
    4. Conversation: every message in order, newest at the bottom. Reply in the box at its foot.

    Opening a conversation marks its replies read. An open one says We usually reply within one business day. A reply from GearLogs Support reaches you in the bell, by email with a link straight to the conversation, and as a number on Support in the side menu. Mark resolved closes a request when it is done, and Reopen brings it back. Writing on a resolved request reopens it too. A reply holds up to 4,000 characters.

Result

Your request reaches a person at GearLogs Support with its own code. Every reply lands in the same conversation, and you are told when one arrives.

If something is refused

  • Add a subject to send. The subject is empty. Send request stays greyed until you write one.
  • Add at least a few words of detail to send. Details needs at least ten characters.
  • The details are over 4000 characters. Shorten the details. Nothing you pasted is cut. The counter under the box shows how many characters it holds.
  • The reply is over 4,000 characters. Shorten your reply. Send waits until it fits.
  • Too many requests for now — please try again a little later. GearLogs limits how many requests and replies go out in an hour. Wait a while and send again.
  • Your message was not sent. Please try again. Your request or reply did not reach GearLogs. Try again in a moment.
  • That conversation is not available to you — it may belong to another member or have been closed. A link from an email led to a request you cannot see. Members see their own requests only.
