Guide 4.5 · Users

Give each member a role, and the rights they need

Add the people who sign in, give each of them a role, grant an extra right where one is needed, and keep an administrator in place.

Before you start

  • The Users screen is for administrators. Only they see it in the side menu.
  • A member here is a login: someone who signs in to GearLogs. The people who hold gear are on the Personnel board.
  • Every login takes a seat. When every seat is in use, Add User is grey. Add seats in Settings › Billing.

  1. Open Users.

    Open Users from the side menu. It opens on User Management.

    1. Add User opens the window for a new login.
    2. Seats in use: your members against the seats your workspace holds.
    3. External ID field label: what your company calls each member’s own ID, such as Employee # or Army ID. The name applies to the whole workspace.
    4. The list: every member by name, with their role, when they were last active and their actions.

  2. Add a member.

    Add User opens Create User.

    1. First Name and Last Name. The first name is required.
    2. External ID, under your own name for it: optional. It shows only on the Users screen, never in logs or analytics.
    3. Email: the address they sign in with.
    4. Password: their first password.

    Choose their role in the same window, then press Save. The new member signs in with this email and password.

  3. Choose a role.

    1. Role: every member has exactly one.
    2. The five roles to choose from.

    Administrator - Full System Access: every right. It is the only role that opens Users and Data Ops. Operator - Manage Data: items, people, tabs and groups, kits and stock; hands gear out and writes it off; sees contact details and everyone’s analytics. User - Assign/Return Only: hands gear out and takes it back, and sees their own analytics. Viewer - Read Only: no rights of its own. Custom - Select Permissions: you tick every right yourself, starting from the rights they had. An operator does not delete items or people, lock stock, export, read everyone’s activity log or open Settings, unless you grant that right.

  4. Grant a right on top of the role.

    Personal Permissions lists every right. Tick a right to grant it to this person on top of their role.

    1. Included in this role: the role’s own rights are ticked and locked. They can never be taken away here.
    2. A right you tick is added for this person only. Here, Write Off / Report Lost Gear on top of User.
    3. Administrators and operators only: Confirm a hand-over on behalf can be added to an operator, never to anyone else.
    4. Administrators only: Manage Users stays with administrators.

    Two rights meet kits. Manage kits builds a kit and its lines. Handing a kit out or taking it back is Assign/Return Assets, as for any hand-out. A loss recorded while taking a kit back needs Write Off / Report Lost Gear. View Contact Details covers each person’s phone, email and notes. Without it, a small lock replaces the contact marks on a person’s card, the details never reach them, and they cannot overwrite them.

  5. Guard your own role, and the Super Administrator.

    Edit User on your own row opens your login.

    1. Role is grey while you are the only administrator. The reason is written under it.
    2. Super Administrator: the only tier that can permanently delete records. Only a super administrator sees this box, and only on an administrator.
    3. An administrator holds every right: each one says Included in this role.

    With another administrator in place, you can give up your own role. Saving asks first: Remove your own administrator role? Save anyway saves it. On another member’s row, Disable Account signs them out and blocks their sign-in until you choose Enable Account. Deleting a login is for a super administrator, and so is setting a new password for a member.

Result

Every member signs in with the rights of their role plus the ones you granted, and the workspace always keeps an administrator.

If something is refused

  • Unable to add a member — every seat this workspace holds is in use. Every seat is in use. Add seats in Settings › Billing, or disable a login that no longer needs access.
  • Username already exists. That email already has a login.
  • You are the only administrator — add another before changing your own role. Written under your own Role while you are the only administrator. Add another administrator first.
  • This is the organization’s only administrator. Assign another administrator before changing or removing this one. A save would leave the workspace with no active administrator, and the workspace itself refuses it.
  • Only a super administrator can delete a login — an administrator can disable it. Delete opens with this reason in red and stays grey. Disable the login instead.
