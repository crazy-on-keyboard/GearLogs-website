Guide 4.18 · Settings

Choose what you are told about

Pick which notices reach you in the app and which by email, and when the emails arrive. Administrators also set where everyone starts.

Before you start

  • Settings › Notifications opens for every member. Everything under My notifications is yours alone.
  • Each switch saves the moment you click it. There is no Save button.
  • Notices about gear reach you only when your login is linked to your staff record. An administrator links it.
  • E-mail allowance and Workspace default are shown to administrators only.

  1. Open Settings › Notifications.

    Open Settings from the side menu, then Notifications in its list.

    1. Settings in the side menu.
    2. Notifications in the Settings list.
    3. E-mail allowance: administrators only. How much of this month’s email allowance is used, and the most the workspace can send.
    4. My notifications: your own choices.

    Security, billing and support emails are never held by the allowance. They are counted apart. When the month’s allowance is used up, routine notices still reach people in the app, and a line on this card says so and offers Add a pack. Workspace default sits lower on the page. Steps 4 and 5 cover it.

  2. Choose, row by row, how each notice reaches you.

    Each row is one kind of notice. The In-app column puts it in the bell. The Email column sends it to your login address, in your language.

    1. The Gear band. In-app and Email at its end name the two columns.
    2. Gear assigned to me: one switch per column. A dimmed switch tagged default follows the workspace default.
    3. An item goes over its storage capacity and An item runs low: two more rows for administrators.
    4. Always on: password, two-factor and recovery changes. These rows have no switch.

    Everyone can switch Gear assigned to me, My return recorded, Gear in my custody written off and New sign-in to my account. Always on for everyone: the security changes, A reply on my support request and A reply I have not opened for three days. Administrators also always get A new administrator is added and Payments, invoices, seats and subscription changes. Out of the box, in-app is on and email is off.

  3. Set your digest and quiet hours.

    Delivery times opens My notifications. It decides when your emails arrive.

    1. Digest at: the hour your daily digest arrives. Workspace follows the workspace’s morning hour. You can also pick your own, from 05:00 to 11:00.
    2. Quiet hours: Workspace, Off or My own. The line under it says what the workspace has set.
    3. One morning email for gear and stock: switch it on under Email. Its In-app column reads email only.

    With the digest on, the gear and stock emails you chose arrive together as one morning email, not one per event. Notices in the bell still arrive at once. Quiet hours hold routine email until they end. Security, billing and support emails never wait. My own adds two hour fields: when your quiet hours start and when they end. Hours that would clash are greyed.

  4. Set the workspace’s delivery times.

    Workspace default sits below My notifications. Administrators see it.

    1. Workspace default: the starting point for every member who has not chosen.
    2. Time zone: every hour on this page is in this zone.
    3. Morning hour: from 05:00 to 11:00. Members’ digests go out at this hour, unless a member picks their own. So does Morning mail to your people.
    4. Quiet hours: switch them on, then set when they start and end. They start as 22:00 to 06:00.

    If your browser is on another zone, a line under Time zone says so, and Use it takes the browser’s zone. A morning hour inside the quiet hours is greyed, and the line under the row says why. Each choice saves the moment you make it.

  5. Set the defaults for everyone.

    Lower in Workspace default, the same rows as in My notifications set where every member starts.

    1. Morning mail to your people: whether the people on your personnel list get a morning email from My Gear when something new waits for them. On out of the box.
    2. One morning email for gear and stock: switch it on to make the digest the workspace default.
    3. Gear assigned to me: each row’s switches set what members who have not chosen get.

    A change here reaches new members and members who have not chosen for themselves. A member’s own choice stays as it is. Once you make a choice of your own, Use workspace defaults appears in My notifications. One click puts your switches, your digest hour and your quiet hours back to the workspace default. It does not ask first.

  6. Read your notices in the bell.

    In-app notices land in the bell in the header, on every screen.

    1. The bell. The number on it counts unread notices. A dash means the count could not be checked yet. It is checked again within a minute.
    2. Mark all read clears the count.
    3. A notice: what happened, and how long ago. An unread one is tinted and carries a dot. Clicking it marks it read.
    4. See all in Support opens Support › Notifications, the full history.

    There, All notifications holds the same rows with no limit, and its own Mark all read. A notice about a support reply opens its conversation. One about a receipt opens Approvals.

Result

Each notice reaches you the way you chose, at the hours you chose. Nothing you did not switch on reaches your inbox, except the always-on rows.

If something is refused

  • Couldn’t save that setting. Please try again. Your pick did not reach GearLogs, so the switch or the hour goes back to what was saved. Try again in a moment.
  • No staff record is linked to this login Gear notices reach you only once an administrator links your login to your staff record. Ask an administrator of your workspace.
  • Our emails to you are bouncing Your address refused our last email, so email notices are paused. In-app notices keep working. Check the address with your administrator.
  • The morning hour 07:00 is inside the stored quiet window, 22:00 to 08:00 — choose a morning hour outside it to turn quiet hours on. The line shows your own hours. The Quiet hours switch stays dimmed until you pick a morning hour outside the window.
  • Your digest hour 07:00 is inside the quiet window 22:00 to 08:00 — choose a digest hour outside it to set your own quiet hours. The line shows your own hours. My own stays dimmed until you pick another Digest at hour.
