Guide 4.3 · Hand-outs

Sign out gear for a team

Give a department head a whole batch of gear in one act. They hold it for the team and sign for it once.

Before you start

  • Issue Batch appears only on the card of a department head. Anyone who holds the right Assign/Return Assets can use it.
  • Marking someone as a department head needs the right Create/Edit Personnel.
  • A sensitive item shows greyed in the list unless you hold Assign Sensitive Gear.

  1. Mark the person as a department head.

    On Personnel, open the person with Edit Record on their card.

    1. Tick Department Head.
    2. Press Save.

    The card turns gold, with a crown, and gains an Issue Batch button.

  2. Find Issue Batch on the head’s card.

    1. The head’s name shows in gold, with a crown beside it.
    2. Issue Batch sits on the card, beside Assign Item.

  3. Open Issue Batch and pick what goes out.

    The batch opens in the same window as any hand-out, with its own tool inside.

    1. The head, held for the team as custodian.
    2. Tick a whole group or tab to add every item in it, or tick items one by one. The search box above the list, Search items, groups or tabs, finds them.
    3. Every ticked item gets a row under What will happen.

  4. Set the quantities.

    1. Every row: all in stock fills every row with all that is in stock.
    2. Type a quantity in a row. It never goes above what is in stock.
    3. All in stock fills that one row.
    4. The Total line counts the items and units as you go.

    After shows how many of each item the head will hold. The ✕ on a row, Remove from the list, takes an item off.

  5. Issue the batch.

    1. The line under the rows says whether the head gets one grouped notice.
    2. Issue 5 items issues everything in one act. The button counts the rows.

    It is all or nothing: if one row cannot go out, nothing is issued. Then Batch issued offers Print manifest or Close. The manifest lists every item, its quantity and its serial units, for the head to sign.

Result

The head holds the batch for the team: returnable gear stays on their card, and consumables are recorded as dispensed. One receipt opens for the whole batch, and the head signs it on My Gear.

If something is refused

  • Denied: Assign Assets You pressed Issue Batch without the right Assign/Return Assets. The button shows dimmed.
  • Pick at least one item Nothing is ticked yet.
  • Not enough stock on one row — it says what is left. Lower the quantity or take the item off the list The stock went down while the window was open. The red row says how many are short; with several rows the sentence counts them.
  • At most 1,000 serial-numbered units in one hand-out — split it into smaller hand-outs The batch holds more than 1,000 serial-numbered units. Issue it in parts.
  • The hand-out was not recorded — nothing changed. Try again The act did not reach the workspace. Nothing was issued.
