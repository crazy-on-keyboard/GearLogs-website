Guide 4.14 · Settings

Make GearLogs yours

Choose your own text size, board layout, action buttons, menu, theme and language. Your choices change only your own screen.

Before you start

  • Settings › Display opens for every member. Everything under My Display is yours alone.
  • Each choice applies the moment you make it. There is no Save button.
  • Text size, groups per row, action buttons and the menu are kept in this browser. Theme and language are kept on your account and follow you to every device.
  • Setting the workspace defaults is for administrators.

  1. Open Settings › Display.

    Open Settings from the side menu, then Display in its list.

    1. Settings in the side menu.
    2. Display in the Settings list.
    3. My Display: your own choices. Nobody else’s screen changes.

    Below it, administrators also see Workspace Default. Step 5 covers it.

  2. Set the size and the layout.

    The first four rows of My Display change how every screen looks to you.

    1. Text Size: from 1, the smallest, to 10, the largest.
    2. Groups Per Row: how many group cards sit across a board row, from 1 to 6. Fewer means wider groups.
    3. Action Buttons: Always shown, or Show on hover to keep busy boards calmer.
    4. Menu: Rail keeps only the icons and folds the names away once you pick a screen. Open keeps the names in sight.

    Drag a slider, or use the arrow keys. The screen changes as you go. With Show on hover, keyboard focus still shows the buttons. On Rail, ☰ opens the names for a moment. Every member starts on Rail.

  3. Pick your theme and language.

    Theme and language are kept on your account, so they follow you to every device.

    1. Theme: five icons, one per theme. The ringed one is yours now.
    2. Language: English or עברית. Hebrew turns the whole app right-to-left. It also sets the language of the emails GearLogs sends you.
    3. Use workspace defaults puts all six choices back. It asks first.

    Spec-Sheet is the page, Light Mode the sun, Dark Mode the moon, Tactical (Army) the shield and Clinical (Medical) the cross. Until you change something, the line Using the workspace default. stands where the button is.

  4. Switch theme or language from the header.

    Theme and language also switch from the header, on every screen, without opening Settings.

    1. The five theme icons. The ringed one is yours now.
    2. The (i) explains the themes.
    3. HE switches the app to Hebrew. In Hebrew the same button reads EN.

    A pick here is the same pick as in My Display: it is saved on your account, and your colleagues keep theirs.

  5. Set the workspace defaults.

    Workspace Default is where every member starts, until they choose their own.

    1. Workspace Default: administrators see this card below My Display.
    2. Text Size and Groups Per Row for the whole workspace. Saved when you let go of the slider.
    3. Action Buttons for the whole workspace. Saved on the click.
    4. Theme and Language for the whole workspace. Saved on the click.

    Members who have not chosen their own follow the new default. A member’s own pick is never overwritten. The menu has no workspace default: every member starts on Rail.

  6. Go back to the workspace defaults.

    Use workspace defaults in My Display asks first.

    1. What goes back: your text size, groups per row, action buttons, menu, theme and language.
    2. Cancel keeps everything as it is.
    3. Use workspace defaults clears all six at once.

    From then on each of them follows the workspace default, and the menu goes back to Rail. Your notifications have their own reset, in Settings › Notifications.

Result

Every screen looks the way you set it. Theme and language follow you to every device, the rest stays with this browser, and nobody else’s screen changes.

If something is refused

  • Couldn’t save that setting. Please try again. Your pick did not reach GearLogs. A theme or language you picked goes back to the one that was saved. Try again in a moment.
