Release Notes

Changelog

Every update to GearLogs, described in plain language — newest first.

GL-22

Field Notes

  • A new Field Notes section brings short, practical reads on tracking equipment and the people who use it — genuinely useful whether or not you use GearLogs
  • Three notes to start, with more to come — find them under Field Notes in the menu
GL-21

A Brand-New Look

  • The GearLogs site has been fully redesigned — a cleaner, more polished experience on a crisp white-paper canvas, with subtle motion throughout
  • See the system working right on the homepage: a live sample of the activity log, exactly as it looks inside GearLogs
GL-20

A More Secure Sign-In

  • Signing in now includes a quick “confirm you’re human” check that blocks automated and bot-driven login attempts before they can reach an account
  • For real people it almost always clears on its own in a moment — nothing new to learn, just added protection around every workspace
GL-19

Noticeably Faster

  • GearLogs now loads and responds noticeably faster across the board — opening screens, saving changes, searching, signing in, and moving between sections all happen more quickly
  • Speed is also steadier — the occasional lag is gone, so the app feels consistently responsive from the first click to the last
GL-18

Full Hebrew, and a Cleaner Look

  • The app now reads fully in Hebrew on every screen — the sign-in and workspace screens, the calendars and date pickers, the confirmation pop-ups and progress windows, search, activity history, analytics, and the backup and maintenance tools all switch with your chosen language, right-to-left
  • Colours stay consistent across every look — progress bars, connection and warning highlights, and import cards now read correctly whether you’re in the light, dark, medical, army, or office theme
  • Clearer, more consistent icons throughout — the Edit button is now a pencil everywhere it used to look like a settings gear, and matching actions such as edit, delete, and assign share the same symbol wherever they appear
  • The Write-Offs register now has the same tidy Actions column as the Personnel and Equipment archives — one clear set of icon buttons, including a History button that opens an item’s activity log, so all three archive tabs work the same way
GL-17

Real Names, Your Own ID Field & Faster Handouts

  • Everyone is shown by their first and last name across the whole app — the user list, activity history, system logs, and analytics — each keeping their short reference code alongside
  • Record your own ID for each person — an employee number, staff ID, or service number — shown to administrators only, and you choose what the field is called so it matches how your organisation talks
  • Hand out gear straight from the board — every item has a quick Assign button that opens a small pop-up right where you are; pick the person, set the amount, and confirm
  • Search instead of scroll — the people and item pickers now filter as you type: find items by code, name, location, or group, and people by ID, name, email, phone, or department
  • Sample data is now a clean on/off switch — a demo only loads into an empty workspace, and removing it clears everything back to empty, so mock data never mixes with your real records; your accounts and licence are always kept
  • An item's lock is now a clear status light on the card, with the on/off switch tucked into the item's own Edit → Actions area
GL-16

Delete Protection & a Consistent Look

  • Permanently erasing anything is now reserved for a Super Administrator — everyday admins keep running the workspace, but wiping a person, an item, a tab or group, or the loss history for good is limited to a super administrator, so nothing important is lost by accident. Every current administrator starts with this ability
  • Only a Super Administrator can grant or withdraw that power, and no one can give it to themselves — the safeguard is enforced behind the scenes and can't be worked around
  • Tighter control over shared areas — dashboard widgets can now only be changed by admins and operators, while everyone else can still see them, and removing a tab or group for good is limited to those allowed to
  • One consistent look for every label — statuses, reasons, and states now read as the same style of coloured tag across the whole app, and quantities show in a matching boxed style, so screens are quicker to scan and line up with the reference codes and activity log
  • Names where you expect them — the loss and personnel dashboard views now show a person's name next to their code, instead of the code alone
  • The same search and filters on every Archive tab — retired people, archived equipment, and write-offs each get the same search, filters, a clear Reset button, and export
GL-15

More Ways to Track Loss

  • Record gear that goes missing or gets damaged on the shelf — before it's ever handed to anyone — so storeroom losses are tracked too, not just gear a person is holding
  • Undo a write-off that shouldn't have happened — the item goes straight back into that person's hands and the loss is cancelled, while the record stays on file marked as voided
  • Write off several of a person's items in one step when more than one thing is lost or damaged, and keep the person on the roster
  • A new dashboard view ranks who has lost the most gear
  • Open a focused loss history for any retired person or archived item straight from the Archive — exactly what was lost, why, and when
GL-14

See Your Losses at a Glance

  • Written-off gear is now flagged right on the card — a person or an item that's lost units shows it at a glance, and opening the card lists exactly what was lost, why, and when
  • New loss dashboards turn write-offs into insight — track losses over time, by reason, by team, and the items lost most, with a running total and a recent-losses feed you can add to any dashboard
  • The Write-Offs register now tells you at a glance whether each loss is against someone still on the roster or already retired
  • Your loss and dispensing history is kept safe through everything — it survives permanently removing a person or item, and restoring from a backup
GL-13

Write-Offs & the Archive

  • Record gear as gone — lost, stolen, damaged, or never returned — without pretending it came back; the count drops and the loss is kept as a permanent record
  • Retire someone who left with their equipment in a single step — write off everything they're holding and archive them together
  • A new Archive area gathers everything you've taken off the board — retired people, archived equipment, and every write-off — in one place, with search, filters, and export
  • Recover a written-off item if it turns up later, and it returns to available stock
  • Restore anything from the Archive, or permanently remove it — the loss records are always kept
GL-12

Clearer Controls & Honest Progress

  • Every action or option that isn't self-explanatory now carries a small info icon — click it for a plain-language note on exactly what it does and what happens, in one consistent place across the app
  • Settings is organised into tabs — System, Display, Branding, Organization Setup, and License — so each area is easy to find
  • Long jobs like deploying sample data or clearing data now show a live progress window that reports each real step as it happens, so you always know it's genuinely working
  • The Data Ops dashboard now shows an honest count of your records instead of an estimated figure
GL-11

Clearer Activity Logs

  • Every log entry now names who performed the action, so it's always clear who did what
  • Reference codes and quantities are colour-coded by type — equipment, people, and users each read distinctly — so the history is easy to scan at a glance
  • The activity view widens to fit longer entries, with nothing cut off
GL-10

Unique Reference Codes

  • Every item, person, group, tab, and team member now carries a short permanent code — shown right on its card — so the same thing is named the same way on every screen
  • Search by code — type a code into the search box to jump straight to that item, person, or record, and filter the activity history the same way
  • Codes run through the history — every recorded action names who did it and what it touched by code, so the log reads clearly and is easy to trace
GL-09

Team Custodian Sign-Out

  • Department heads can now sign out a batch of gear in one step and hold it all under their name for the team — pick everything from a checklist, set quantities, and confirm once
GL-08

Rearrange Your Tabs

  • Put your tabs in any order — open a tab's Edit screen and use Move Backward or Move Forward to slot it exactly where you want, the same way groups already move
GL-07

Clearer Boards & Safer Edits

  • Colour-coded tabs that wrap — when you have many tabs they now wrap onto rows instead of scrolling off-screen, and each tab carries its own colour so they're easy to tell apart at a glance
  • Click-to-expand cards — click a person or item card to open its details, and click the card again to close it
  • One menu per group — every group action now lives in a single settings menu instead of being split in two
  • Safer deletes — you can no longer accidentally remove a tab or group that still has things inside it; a clear message explains what to clear out first, and deleting an empty one asks you to confirm
  • Consistent edit screens — editing a tab, group, item, or person now shares the same clear "Edit" heading
GL-06

Personal Display, Activity History & Smarter Dashboards

  • Workspace display defaults — admins can set the default text size and how many groups sit across a row for the whole workspace, while each person can still personalise their own view
  • Activity history everywhere — a Logs button on any person, group, or tab opens a scrollable record of what has been assigned, returned, and dispensed, loaded in pages and kept for twelve months
  • Editable dashboard widgets with live preview — adjust any existing widget, and see exactly what a widget will look like as you build it
  • Richer demo data — deploying a sample sector now fills it with weeks of realistic activity and a ready-made analytics dashboard, so you can explore the whole app straight away
  • Analytics restyled to match the app's spec-sheet look
GL-05

Interface Consistency & Reliability

  • A unified interface — buttons, status tags, and page headers now follow one consistent style across every screen
  • Full theme support — every screen, chart, and control re-tints correctly when you switch between the office, dark, army and medical themes
  • Clearer activity log — entries now read with real names instead of internal codes, and duplicate lines are gone
  • More honest status — screens now tell you plainly when something couldn't load, instead of a misleading "all clear"
  • Steadier controls — action buttons guard against accidental double-clicks, so nothing runs twice
  • Refined details — consistent date and time formatting, tidier in-control help via the click-to-open information icon, and a cleaner, more accessible look throughout
  • Behind-the-scenes engineering improvements for long-term reliability and faster future updates
GL-04

Live Collaboration & Admin Controls

  • Real-time sync — changes now appear instantly across everyone's screens, no refresh needed
  • Team presence — see who is currently active in your workspace at a glance
  • Live dashboards — analytics, activity feeds, and system health now read from your real data and update as it changes
  • Admin account controls — disable or re-enable a member's access, and reset a member's password, from the admin panel
  • License-aware safety — when a license lapses, the workspace switches to view-and-export only until it's renewed, so nothing is lost
  • More dashboard widgets — trend lines, lists, department tables, and quick actions, all built from your live data
  • Built-in error log — administrators can review any captured issues in one place, each with its own reference
GL-03

Board Redesign & Reliability Update

  • Redesigned board — groups auto-fit the space in a clean grid, long names wrap instead of being cut off, and item rows are decluttered
  • New per-user display controls — adjust the text size and how many groups sit across a row
  • Calmer help — hover tooltips are gone; a small click-to-open information icon explains the controls that need it, only when you ask
  • Archive instead of delete — remove an item or person from the board while keeping their full history, and restore them any time from the group's Archived section
  • Richer sample data — deploy a realistic sector organisation (office, medical, IT, army, logistics or manufacturing) to explore or demo the whole app in one click
  • More dependable stock handling — quantities stay correct even when several people are working at the same time
  • More reliable CSV import — no more accidental duplicate locations or departments
  • Safer restore — an interrupted "restore from backup" now rolls back cleanly instead of leaving your data half-loaded
  • Clearer, more honest labels across Settings and Data Operations
  • A faster, smoother board with a refreshed look and the Blueprint Mono typeface
