Frequently Asked Questions

The things people ask most often. Still stuck? Send us a message.

What types of organizations use GearLogs?

GearLogs is designed for any organization that needs to track equipment, manage personnel, and control asset assignments. This includes logistics operations, medical facilities, field units, IT departments, and manufacturing operations.

How do I get started?

Send us a message from the contact form and we'll set up your organization. Once provisioned, you can sign in at app.gearlogs.com, configure your departments and groups, and begin adding items and personnel.

Is GearLogs fast and responsive?

Yes. GearLogs is built to feel quick — screens open, changes save, and searches return without waiting around, and it stays consistently responsive as your inventory and activity history grow.

Does every item and person get an ID?

Yes. GearLogs gives every item, person, group, tab, and team member a short permanent reference code, shown right on its card. You can search by that code to jump straight to something, and it runs through the activity log so it's always clear exactly what each entry refers to.

Can I record our own ID numbers for people?

Yes. Alongside the automatic reference code, you can record each person's own identifier — an employee number, staff ID, or service number — and you choose what that field is called so it matches how your organisation talks. It's shown to administrators on the user list, and is deliberately kept out of the activity log and analytics.

How do I know what a button does before I use it?

Any action or setting that isn't obvious carries a small info icon beside it. Click it for a short, plain-language note on exactly what it does and what will happen — so consequential actions like backups, restores, or clearing data are never a guess. Longer jobs also show a live progress window that reports each step as it runs.

Can I import existing data?

Yes. GearLogs supports bulk CSV import for items, personnel, and users. The import engine automatically detects duplicates and queues conflicts for your review.

Is my data secure?

Yes. All data is encrypted, isolated per organization, and protected by role-based access controls. Sign-in is guarded by an automated human-verification check that blocks bot-driven login attempts, and every action is logged in a complete audit trail.

Why do I see a “verify you’re human” step when I sign in?

It’s a quick automated-abuse check that protects accounts from bot-driven and scripted sign-in attempts. For real people it almost always clears on its own in a moment — there’s nothing extra to do.

Does it support multiple languages?

GearLogs currently supports English and Hebrew (with full RTL support). Additional languages can be added on request.

Are my settings saved for just me, or the whole team?

It depends on the setting. How your board is arranged — the order of tabs and groups, group sizes, and your analytics dashboards — is shared across the whole workspace, so everyone sees the same layout. Your personal display comfort — text size and how many groups fit across a row — is saved just for you, in your own browser, starting from a default your administrator sets.

Can I customize the dashboard?

Yes. Build your own analytics dashboard from a range of widget types — activity trends, stock levels, live feeds, breakdowns and more — and add, edit, resize, and preview each widget as you build it.

Can I recover something I removed?

Yes. Retired people and archived equipment live in a dedicated Archive area — search it, and restore anything back to the board in a click. A written-off item that later turns up can be recovered straight back into available stock. Tabs and groups are protected too: you can't remove one that still has things inside it, and removing an empty one asks you to confirm first.

Who can permanently delete records?

Only a Super Administrator. Everyday administrators run the workspace — assigning gear, editing, archiving, writing off, and managing users — but permanently erasing anything for good, whether a person, an item, a tab or group, or the loss history, is reserved for a Super Administrator, so nothing important is wiped by accident. Every existing administrator starts with this ability; only a Super Administrator can grant or remove it, and no one can give it to themselves.

What happens to gear that's lost, damaged, or never returned?

You can write it off — record it as lost, stolen, damaged, or unreturned, without pretending it came back. The item's total count drops and the loss is kept as a permanent record, so your numbers stay honest. When someone leaves with equipment they never returned, you can write off everything they're holding and retire them in one step. Every write-off, and everyone who's been retired, is gathered in a single Archive area with search, filters, and export. Losses are easy to see: a flag appears right on the person and item cards — open one for its full loss history — loss dashboards chart the trends by reason, team and item, and the register shows whether each loss is against someone still active or already retired.

Is there an activity history?

Yes. A Logs button on any person, group, or tab opens a running record of what's been assigned, returned, and dispensed — kept for twelve months and searchable. Every entry names who performed the action, and reference codes and quantities are colour-coded by type so the history reads at a glance.

Can my team work in GearLogs at the same time?

Yes. GearLogs syncs in real time — changes made by one person appear for everyone right away, and you can see who else is active in the workspace.

What do I need to run it?

Nothing to install. GearLogs runs in any modern web browser — just sign in and go.
