Section 1

Capabilities

1.1

Inventory Management

Organize items by departments and groups. Monitor stock levels against configurable thresholds. Manage consumable and durable equipment separately with per-item controls.

1.2

Personnel Management

Maintain personnel records with department assignments, designations, and contact information — each person shown by name, with an optional ID field of your own (employee number, staff ID, or service number). Track who holds what equipment and their consumption history.

1.3

Asset Assignment

Assign equipment to personnel with quantity tracking — straight from the board with a quick Assign button and a searchable person picker. Process returns and dispense consumables. A department head can sign out a whole batch and hold it for the team. Full history per person with automatic stock adjustment.

1.4

Reference Codes

Every item, person, group, tab, and team member carries a short permanent code, shown on its card and searchable everywhere — woven through the activity log so it's always clear what each entry refers to.

1.5

Colour-Coded Activity Log

Every action is recorded and colour-coded by type: who performed it, what they touched, and how much. Search and filter by multiple criteria, and export logs for compliance and review.

1.6

Real-Time Collaboration

Your whole team can work at once. Changes appear for everyone instantly with no refresh, and you can see who's active in the workspace at a glance.

1.7

Analytics & Dashboard

Configurable dashboard with multiple widget types. Activity trends, low-stock alerts, live feeds, and export-ready reports. Add, edit, resize, and preview widgets to see what matters most.

1.8

Data Operations

Import data from CSV with intelligent deduplication. Export reports for audits, rosters, and compliance. Archive and restore items and people instead of losing them. Encrypted backups for data protection. Long jobs show a live progress window that reports every real step.

1.9

Guided & Clear

Every action or setting that isn't self-explanatory carries a small info icon — one click reveals what it does and what will happen. Statuses, reasons, and quantities read as the same colour-coded labels across every screen, settings are organised into clean tabs, and dashboards show honest figures rather than estimates, so nothing consequential is ever a guess.

1.10

Write-Offs & Archive

Record gear that's lost, stolen, damaged, or never returned — the count drops and the loss is kept as a permanent record. Retire someone who left with their equipment in a single step. A dedicated Archive gathers retired people, archived equipment, and every write-off in one place, with search, filters, and export — restore anything, or recover a written-off item back into stock. Losses stay visible where you work: flagged right on the person and item cards with a full history, charted on loss dashboards by reason, team and item, and the register shows whether each loss is against active or retired personnel.

Section 2

How It Works

I

Set Up Your Organization

Configure departments, groups, and locations to match your organizational structure. Start from scratch or use a pre-built template.

II

Add Your Data

Add items and personnel manually or bulk-import from CSV. Duplicates are detected automatically and queued for your review.

III

Assign & Track

Assign equipment to personnel. Dispense consumables. Process returns. Everything is logged and tracked in real time.

IV

Monitor & Report

Build your dashboard. Track trends. Receive alerts for low stock. Export reports for audits and compliance reviews.

Section 3

Security & Trust

3.1

Data Isolation

Each organization's data is completely isolated. Users can only access data within their own organization. Multi-tenant architecture with strict enforcement at every layer.

3.2

Role-Based Access

Fine-grained permission system ensures users see only what they need. Administrators control who can view, edit, and manage each area of the system. Permanently erasing records is reserved for a Super Administrator, so nothing irreversible happens by accident.

3.3

Encrypted & Secure

All data encrypted in transit and at rest. Industry-standard authentication with secure session management. Backups encrypted for additional protection.

3.4

Audit Everything

Complete audit trail of every action. Know who did what, when. Export-ready logs for compliance requirements and internal reviews.

3.5

Sign-In Protection

An automated human-verification check at sign-in blocks bot-driven and scripted login attempts before they reach an account. For real people it clears in a moment, with nothing extra to do.
