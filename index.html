Section 1

Capabilities

1.1

Inventory Management

Organize items by departments and groups. Monitor stock levels against configurable thresholds. Manage consumable and durable equipment separately with per-item controls.

1.2

Personnel Management

Maintain personnel records with department assignments, designations, and contact information. Track who holds what equipment and their consumption history.

1.3

Asset Assignment

Assign equipment to personnel with quantity tracking. Process returns and track consumable dispensation. Full assignment history per person with automatic stock adjustment.

1.4

Analytics & Dashboard

Configurable dashboard with multiple widget types. Activity trends, low-stock alerts, live feeds, and export-ready reports. Customize your view to see what matters most.

1.5

Data Operations

Import data from CSV with intelligent deduplication. Export reports for audits, rosters, and compliance. Encrypted backups for data protection.

1.6

Complete Audit Trail

Every operation logged with timestamp and user. Search and filter by multiple criteria. Export audit logs for compliance and review.

Section 3

Security & Trust

3.1

Data Isolation

Each organization's data is completely isolated. Users can only access data within their own organization. Multi-tenant architecture with strict enforcement at every layer.

3.2

Role-Based Access

Fine-grained permission system ensures users see only what they need. Administrators control who can view, edit, and manage each area of the system.

3.3

Encrypted & Secure

All data encrypted in transit and at rest. Industry-standard authentication with secure session management. Backups encrypted for additional protection.

3.4

Audit Everything

Complete audit trail of every action. Know who did what, when. Export-ready logs for compliance requirements and internal reviews.

Section 4

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of organizations use GearLogs?

GearLogs is designed for any organization that needs to track equipment, manage personnel, and control asset assignments. This includes logistics operations, medical facilities, field units, IT departments, and manufacturing operations.

How do I get started?

Contact us to set up your organization. Once provisioned, you can sign in at app.gearlogs.com, configure your departments and groups, and begin adding items and personnel.

Can I import existing data?

Yes. GearLogs supports bulk CSV import for items, personnel, and users. The import engine automatically detects duplicates and queues conflicts for your review.

Is my data secure?

Yes. All data is encrypted, isolated per organization, and protected by role-based access controls. Every action is logged in a complete audit trail.

Does it support multiple languages?

GearLogs currently supports English and Hebrew (with full RTL support). Additional languages can be added on request.
