Section 1

Capabilities

1.1

Inventory Management

Track items organized by departments and groups. Monitor stock levels against configurable health thresholds. Lock critical assets. Manage consumable and durable equipment separately.

  • Stock health thresholds (low / healthy / total)
  • Admin lock on item stock levels
  • Consumable vs. durable distinction
  • Location and group tracking
1.2

Personnel Management

Maintain complete personnel records with government IDs, designations, department assignments, and contact information. Track who holds what and their consumption history.

  • Full personnel records with IDs and contact
  • Primary and secondary department assignment
  • Designation and rank tracking
  • Department head identification
1.3

Asset Assignment

Assign equipment to personnel with quantity tracking. Process partial returns. Track consumable dispensation separately with one-way logging. Full assignment history per person.

  • Assign and return with quantity control
  • Consumable dispensation logging
  • Per-person asset holding view
  • Automatic stock adjustment
1.4

Analytics & Dashboard

Configurable dashboard with 16 widget types. 90-day activity trends per group. Low-stock alerts. Activity feeds. Custom widget builder. Export-ready reports.

  • 16 widget types (stat cards, charts, feeds, alerts)
  • Customizable grid layout (4-column)
  • 90-day trend analysis
  • Time range filtering
1.5

Data Operations

Bulk import from CSV with intelligent deduplication. Conflict resolution for duplicates. Encrypted backup and restore. Multiple export formats for audits, rosters, and inventory reports.

  • CSV import with dedup engine
  • Conflict resolution (discard, overwrite, copy, repair)
  • AES-GCM encrypted backup/restore
  • CSV export (audit, roster, assignments, users)
1.6

Audit Trail

Every operation logged with timestamp, user, and action description. Filter logs by user, action type, date range, and keywords. Export-ready. User last-active tracking.

  • Comprehensive activity logging
  • Search and filter by multiple criteria
  • CSV log export
  • User activity timestamps

Section 3

Operational Workflow

I

Organization Setup

Create your organization. Deploy a sector template or start from scratch. Configure departments, groups, and locations to match your structure.

II

Populate Data

Add items and personnel manually or bulk-import from CSV. The dedup engine prevents duplicates. Conflicts are queued for review.

III

Assign & Track

Assign equipment to personnel. Dispense consumables. Process returns. Every transaction is logged with full audit trail.

IV

Monitor & Report

Configure your dashboard. Track trends over 90 days. Receive low-stock alerts. Export reports for audits and compliance.
