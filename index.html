Section 5

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of organizations use GearLogs?

GearLogs is designed for any organization that needs to track equipment, manage personnel, and control asset assignments. This includes logistics operations, medical facilities, field units, IT departments, and manufacturing operations.

How do I get started?

Contact us to set up your organization. Once provisioned, you can sign in at app.gearlogs.com, configure your departments and groups, and begin adding items and personnel.

Can I import existing data?

Yes. GearLogs supports bulk CSV import for items, personnel, and users. The import engine automatically detects duplicates and queues conflicts for your review.

Is my data secure?

Yes. All data is encrypted, isolated per organization, and protected by role-based access controls. Every action is logged in a complete audit trail.

Does it support multiple languages?

GearLogs currently supports English and Hebrew (with full RTL support). Additional languages can be added on request.

Are my settings saved for just me, or the whole team?

It depends on the setting. How your board is arranged — the order of tabs and groups, group sizes, and your analytics dashboards — is shared across the whole workspace, so everyone sees the same layout. Your personal display comfort — text size and how many groups fit across a row — is saved just for you, in your own browser, starting from a default your administrator sets.

Can I customize the dashboard?

Yes. Build your own analytics dashboard from a range of widget types — activity trends, stock levels, live feeds, breakdowns and more — and add, edit, resize, and preview each widget as you build it.

Can I recover something I removed?

Items and personnel can be archived instead of deleted, and restored later from the archive. Tabs and groups are protected by safeguards: you can't remove one that still has things inside it, and removing an empty one asks you to confirm first.

Is there an activity history?

Yes. A Logs button on any person, group, or tab opens a running record of what's been assigned, returned, and dispensed — kept for twelve months and searchable, so you always have an audit trail.

Can my team work in GearLogs at the same time?

Yes. GearLogs syncs in real time — changes made by one person appear for everyone right away, and you can see who else is active in the workspace.

What do I need to run it?

Nothing to install. GearLogs runs in any modern web browser — just sign in and go.
